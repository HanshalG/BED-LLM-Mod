\title{When Does Non-Myopic Bayesian Experimental Design Work with Language Models?}
\author{Hanshal Goyal}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Sequential Bayesian experimental design (BED) offers a principled way for
language-model agents to ask questions and plan future information gathering.
Yet deeper planning can optimize errors in an LLM-derived model rather than
useful information. We separate two regimes. In finite Rock Diagnosis, an LLM
proposes contingent policies and an exact verifier scores them; across five
preregistered maps, depth-two StrategyEIG beats shared-root myopic, exhaustive
one-step, and matched-random controls, with all 15 paired intervals positive.
The LLM remains replaceable there. We therefore test an LLM-native mechanism on
$\tau$-Knowledge: GPT-5.4 generates path-dependent information-need hypotheses
and retrieval trees over 698 banking documents, while hidden required-document
annotations provide external endpoints. An initial 20-task study improves
target-blind first-root ranking ($.779$ versus $.686$, $p=.0398$) but fails its
structural and continuation gates. After iterative development, a frozen
count-dominant receding policy passes every gate on an independent untouched
20-task holdout: root accuracy is $.702$ versus $.554$ ($p=.0352$), continuation
accuracy is $.757$ ($p<10^{-4}$), and exact coverage is 30 documents versus 26
myopic. A preregistered fresh execution on the same tasks reverses root accuracy
($.558$ versus $.628$) and ties myopic coverage (23 each); the task-clustered
two-execution gain is $+.10$ documents ($p=.348$). Thus the LLM-native mechanism
is promising but not execution-robust. Negative location, Paprika, MediQ, and
MovieLens audits localize why deeper semantic planning otherwise fails.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Interactive agents must often acquire information before committing to an
answer or action. A clinical agent asks about symptoms; a support agent diagnoses
a fault; a guessing agent narrows a large semantic space. Direct prompting is an
unreliable solution: MediQ reports that asking models to interact can reduce
accuracy relative to answering from the initial context \citep{li2024mediq}.
Bayesian experimental design instead selects a query by its expected information
gain (EIG) about a target variable. BED-LLM makes this practical in semantic
spaces by using an LLM to propose hypotheses and provide response likelihoods
\citep{choudhury2025bedllm}.

The natural next step is non-myopic BED: score a query by both its immediate EIG
and the information available to a future adaptive query. Recent methods simulate
conversation trees for this purpose \citep{hu2024uot,arnould2026cabed}. In
principle this can value a prerequisite question with little immediate gain. In
practice every additional branch invokes the same uncertain model, changes the
belief on which later calls condition, and maximizes over more noisy continuation
scores. A larger tree can therefore make a confidently worse decision.

We investigate this distinction using a validation-first sequence. Exact
constrained location verifies the recursion, and Rock Diagnosis combines enabling
movement, LLM-generated policies, and exact verification. To test a setting where
the LLM is not replaceable by finite search, $\tau$-Knowledge supplies an
unstructured corpus and externally annotated retrieval endpoints
\citep{shi2026tau}. Semantic 20 Questions, natural location, MovieLens, Paprika,
and MediQ then expose failures in horizon value, ranking, utility, and model
sufficiency.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \setlength{\topsep}{0.25em}
  \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item StrategyEIG, an LLM-Modulo sequential BED architecture in which an LLM
  proposes explicit contingent policies and an exact Bayesian component verifies
  their total information gain \citep{kambhampati2024llmmodulo};
  \item preregistered paired evidence that both the LLM proposal prior and
  non-myopic verification are load-bearing in a depth-favorable Rock geometry;
  \item a target-blind external-benchmark study in which one held-out execution
  improves root and continuation ranking, while a preregistered fresh execution
  exposes instability in LLM-generated trees and scores;
  \item a cross-environment audit that separates correctness of the planning
  recursion from validity of the probabilistic interaction model;
  \item two external-benchmark autopsies with invalid outcomes quarantined rather
  than repurposed as method evidence; and
  \item a concrete validation contract for future non-myopic LLM BED: target and
  latent sufficiency, calibrated likelihoods, branch/deployment equivalence, a
  verified greedy gap, and ranking fidelity before policy evaluation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Probabilistic Contract}

Let $h_t=((x_1,y_1),\ldots,(x_t,y_t))$ be the interaction history and
$\theta$ the finite target decoded at the endpoint. A generative state $z$ may
contain additional information needed to predict responses, with
$\theta=g(z)$. The relevant model is
\[
 p_t(\theta,z)\,p(y\mid z,x,h_t), \qquad
 p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)=\sum_z p(y\mid z,x,h_t)p_t(z\mid\theta).
\]
When $\theta$ itself is sufficient, as for a hidden animal answering a property
question, $z$ can be omitted. When $\theta$ is merely a decision label, such as
``treat hypoperfusion first,'' it does not determine a patient's glucose or
history and cannot serve as the response-generating state.

For a coherent model, one-step EIG is
\[
 I_t(x)=\sum_{\theta,y}p_t(\theta)p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)
 \log\frac{p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)}{\sum_{\theta'}p_t(\theta')
 p(y\mid\theta',x,h_t)}.
\]
Our exact depth-two score is
\[
 Q_2(x)=I_t(x)+\mathbb E_{y\mid x,h_t}
 \left[\max_{x'\in\mathcal X(h_t,x,y)} I_{t+1}(x')\right].
\]
It branches over every positive-mass response, performs a Bayesian update on the
fixed target support, generates branch-specific follow-ups, and adds the best
second-step EIG. This is mathematically appropriate for the model supplied to it.
It cannot determine whether that model describes deployment.

Two requirements are therefore distinct. \emph{Model validity} asks whether the
target, latent state, likelihood, update, and simulated branches form one process
that matches deployment. \emph{Planning value} asks whether the task contains
complementarity or gating such that a lower-immediate-EIG query enables a more
informative continuation. If information has adaptive diminishing returns,
greedy policies can already be competitive \citep{golovin2011adaptive}. Depth
needs a demonstrated gap, not merely a multi-turn interface.

\section{Audit Design and Evidence Rules}

We aggregate only preregistered or reconstructable paired evidence. Exact
location tests use the known source prior and signal likelihood. The animals
audit reconstructs every 20-round binary trial and reports deterministic
bootstrap intervals. Paprika and MediQ use hash-pinned official releases and
strict transcript audits. Failed parsing, unsupported simulator facts, and
endpoint contradictions fail closed.

Most importantly, we distinguish \emph{policy evidence} from \emph{diagnostic
evidence}. A Paprika run whose customer reports the private correct remedy as
failing cannot compare policies, even if aggregate resolution numbers exist.
A MediQ run with an invalid likelihood cannot establish EIG value, even if its
queries are fluent. Such runs may localize a broken link in
Figure~\ref{fig:validation-chain}; they do not support an accuracy claim.

\section{Results Across Environments}

\paragraph{Exact lookahead can work.}
We first constructed a branch-decoy source prior with a local-bump observation
model and movement-constrained queries. The first query can reveal which region
to enter, while movement limits make that choice affect later measurements. Over
120 paired trials, exact depth two reduced final RMSE from $0.5606$ for greedy
EIG to $0.1529$, a mean paired difference of $-0.4077$. This is a harness result,
not an external-benchmark claim. It shows that the implementation and objective
can exploit a genuine delayed-information structure when model error is absent.

\paragraph{One-step semantic BED transfers; depth does not yet.}
On animals 20 Questions, depth-one EIG achieved accuracy AUC $0.743$ versus
$0.465$ for a thinking naive policy and $0.458$ for non-thinking naive over 40
paired trials. The AUC gain over thinking naive was $0.278$ with 95\% bootstrap
interval $[0.191,0.365]$. This is the stable positive BED result.

The apparent aggregate depth-two AUC was $0.770$, but the full depth-one and
depth-two streams were not paired. On the only exact ten-trial seed block,
depth two minus depth one was $-0.040$ with interval $[-0.105,0.025]$ and
3/1/6 wins/ties/losses. Depth three was worse than depth two across 40 paired
trials: $-0.126$ $[-0.224,-0.035]$. Thus these data support semantic EIG, not
non-myopic improvement.

Natural location experiments reinforce this distinction. In a 30-trial
movement-constrained study using exact physical likelihoods but LLM-generated
strategies, final RMSE was $0.412$ for greedy EIG versus $0.929$, $1.127$, and
$0.542$ for horizons 1, 3, and 5. Unconstrained horizons were flat; depth was
non-monotonic and naive remained competitive.

\paragraph{LLM-native semantic lookahead improves both links.}
Rock's verifier is exact, so we next used the official $\tau$-Knowledge banking
domain: 698 unstructured policy documents, deterministic BM25 top-3 transitions,
and hidden required-document annotations \citep{shi2026tau}. From only a
customer's first utterance, GPT-5.4 generated eight information-need hypotheses,
five first searches, then refreshed its needs and generated four
document-conditioned follow-ups per root. This belief/search state is semantic,
regenerated, and path-dependent; required-document labels were never shown.

Two isolated target-blind root scorers used identical trees. The myopic view saw
only first results; the non-myopic view saw complete depth-two branches. In an
initial untouched 20-task study, oracle-tail root accuracy increased from $.6858$
to $.7788$ (exact task-level $p=.0398$, bootstrap gain interval
$[.0043,.1869]$). Root coverage rose from 39 to 44 documents, but the overall
gate failed: a non-myopic gap appeared on only 2/20 tasks, and the joint scorer's
own follow-ups realized 34 rather than 44 documents.

We used that split only for further development. The final receding scorer hides
candidate query wording, treats refreshed needs as fallible, and asks GPT-5.4 to
classify which newly returned documents support the original or regenerated
needs. Distinct useful-document count dominates its tie-break. A format-only
V3.1 amendment accepting ``02'' as the same score as ``2'' was frozen before
holdout access and is disclosed because it superseded a prior no-repair promise.

On all 20 remaining no-script tasks, selected blindly before development, oracle
and immediate-greedy roots differed on 8/20 and a positive non-myopic gap
appeared on 6/20 (mean $.35$ documents). Full-tree root accuracy was $.7025$
versus $.5537$ myopic (121 pairs; exact $p=.0352$, gain interval
$[.0161,.2963]$). Receding continuation accuracy was $.7566$ (228 pairs;
task-clustered chance $p=6.9{\times}10^{-5}$), with 80/100 oracle-optimal choices
and mean regret $.21$. The policy retained 30 of 33 documents available under
its selected roots, versus 26 for myopic receding, 25 for the original joint
selector, and 22 for seeded random; all frozen gates passed. Exact endpoint
tests remain underpowered ($p=.25,.0898,.0774$, respectively), so this execution
supports LLM-owned ranking plus directional end-to-end gain. We treat fixed-tree
semantic ranking as primary; coverage is a noisier execution check. Post-hoc
controls retrieved 22/21/17 documents versus 30. Across two disjoint
blocks, full-minus-myopic score ranked exact future-only gain at $.618/.668$
(pooled $.644$, 187 pairs, $p=.0012$); myopic/full scores ranked
immediate/total value at $.726/.739$. Across six independent fixed-tree scorer
runs, directly optimizing this difference was stable (root agreement $.73$)
but retrieved only $22.3$ documents versus $26.8$ myopic and $30.7$ under the
raw full score. The difference isolates future signal but is not a total BED
objective. Belief-only shuffles were null/adverse and subtree replay directional
($+.054$, $p=.228$). Thus semantic future value is load-bearing;
refreshed-belief causality remains unverified.

A preregistered same-task replication regenerated every tree and score in 280
clean calls. Root accuracy reversed ($.5575$ non-myopic versus $.6283$ myopic);
continuation remained $.7220$, but coverage tied at 23. The two-execution
within-task average favors non-myopia by $.10$ documents, yet is inconclusive
($p=.3484$, interval $[-.275,.450]$); Holm-corrected rank metrics are also null.
A preregistered three-call scorer ensemble on fixed trees retained a $+.120$
root-ranking gain and $+4$ documents, but failed its loss cap (4/20). Thus the
first execution shows possibility and fixed-tree scorer fidelity, not
reliability under full tree regeneration.

An orthogonal public-Zendo smoke generated executable particles and all eight
refreshed branches. On rule $\mu$, blinded readiness sacrificed $.316$ immediate
nats yet gained $.097$ behavioral agreement over myopic and fixed d2
($\rho=.80$). This is exploratory: two seven-rule confirmations stopped before
endpoints on malformed outputs.

\paragraph{LLM proposals plus exact lookahead work in enabling geometry.}
The negative natural-location sweep did not test the architecture where depth
itself was useful: a later exact width gate showed that its smooth sensing geometry
favored one-step action coverage. We therefore moved to finite Rock Diagnosis.
Movement has exactly zero immediate EIG, but changes distance-dependent sensor
accuracy. The LLM returned six explicit two-action branch policies per decision:
three distinct movement roots followed by checks, and three direct-check roots.
A finite exact posterior and exhaustive observation branching scored every policy;
the LLM was never called inside rollout scoring.

We preregistered 30 paired eight-round trajectories on each of two paper maps,
then scale extensions on the canonical eight-rock RockSample[7,8] geometry from
\citet{smith2004hsvi}, the standard eleven-rock RockSample[11,11] SARSOP instance,
and a frozen fifteen-rock RockSample[15,15] POBAX instance.
This is a diagnosis-only
adaptation: it retains the latent rock vector, grid movement, and distance-dependent
sensor, while excluding sampling, reward, and exit actions.
Controls remove one component at a time: shared d1 scores the identical LLM roots
myopically; width uses exhaustive d1 over all legal actions with matched exact-scorer
units and one LLM ordering call; random strategy uses the same branch grammar and
exact d2 verifier with random legal policies. Table~\ref{tab:rock-strategy} reports
paired gains in mean posterior-entropy AUC; positive is better for StrategyEIG.

The mechanism is direct: StrategyEIG moves to enable accurate checks while both
myopic controls never move. Across the five maps its bounded proposals recover
90.6\%, 86.1\%, 85.8\%, 73.9\%, and 41.1\% of exhaustive d2 value. Beating
shared d1 makes lookahead load-bearing; beating matched random strategies makes
the LLM proposal prior load-bearing. Exact closure of eight-rock roots raised
recovery from 85.8\% to 99.6\%, but optimal-root coverage was 242/270, one state
below the frozen paid-run gate, so it was not tuned or run.

Independent GPT-5.4 Mini and Gemma replications passed entropy and truth-log
controls at eight rocks. Four Gemma seeds and GPT-5.4 Mini did likewise at
11 rocks (2,048 states); a K4 width saturated K6 quality with 9.49 rather than
14.24 scorer nodes per decision. At 15 rocks (32,768 states), three Gemma seeds
pooled gains of .6839, .6815, and .6760 against shared d1, width, and random;
GPT-5.4 Mini independently passed all six gates. K4 used 9.60 nodes per decision
versus 616.94 for exhaustive d2, a registered tree-width rather than wall-clock
comparison.

Proposal capacity remains necessary. Non-thinking E4B recovered only 6.8\% of
exhaustive d2 and failed matched-width and truth-log gates. A non-thinking 12B
model passed all gates across three seeds, recovering 49.5--53.3\%; different
seeds make this a descriptive E4B-to-12B boundary, not a causal size comparison.
Exact verification removes scoring noise but cannot recover a useful branch
absent from the bounded proposal set.

A Gated Sensor control had a large exact d2 gap, but its GPT-5.4 Mini proposal
tied random because it activated redundantly at the terminal step. Verification
cannot recover setup value absent from the proposal.

Three data-derived unlocks were more favorable. On Mushroom, grounded GPT
collected 50/50 with zero projected branches, exactly matched d2, beat d1 and
random, and truth-log intervals agreed. Cleveland recovered 1.009 of exact d2
with zero projected branches and positive entropy AUC, but its independent
truth-log interval against random crossed zero, so it is not an all-gates
confirmation. Thyroid's native delayed-assay metadata produced an exact d2 gap.
Names-only scoring exposed ungrounded empirical assay utility; a utility-card
repair failed before endpoints. A projection-only ablation collected 0/50 with
projected branches and recovered .432. Bounded utility then recovered .994 and
beat d1 and matched-random continuations, but remains machine-grounded rather
than unaided LLM planning.

Deeper receding horizon was not automatically better: terminal-EIG d3 repeated
a noisy check and lost to d2. A range-gated task made depth load-bearing: Exact d3 beat exact d2
by .4630, and 26B recovered the route on 16/16 cells, but a
tied-control identity audit left no trajectory, only proposal evidence. The
exact d4 policy then gained .3484 (500/0/0); score-free models produced reasoning-only stops,
while semantic target selection recovered 10/10 routes before provider
truncation---mechanism evidence, not an LLM-policy claim. A focused prior yielded
an exact h5 gain (500/500); a compiler selected the route on 15/16 cells with
.937 recovery. Its initial 50-pair run matched exhaustive d5, and three
additional preregistered replications gained .2727 over identical compiled plans
at h4 (150/0/0) and .2118 over matched-random h5 (148/2/0). Replay used 51
physical prompts for 1,200 logical decisions.

\paragraph{Paprika fails before a horizon claim.}
Paprika troubleshooting mixes information-gathering questions with corrective
actions that change and may terminate the world \citep{tajwar2025curious}. Pure
EIG values discrimination among cause/remedy strings, not the probability of
resolution minus action cost. A remedy that resolves under every hypothesis can
have zero EIG, while a diagnostic that identifies the cause but cannot resolve
the task can score highly.

The implementation also used fixed support and synthetic outcome labels in
lookahead while deployment refreshed support, observed free text, and could
terminate. A ten-task full-depth diagnostic used 70,004 requests versus 3,365
for one-step EIG and resolved 2 versus 3 tasks. Those counts are not policy
evidence: later manual audit found a customer response claiming that the exact
private remedy had failed. A separate 50-task arbitration evaluation was also
quarantined by endpoint review. The defensible result is the failure mechanism,
not which arm won an invalid simulator contest.

A separate target-hidden support diagnostic removed that endpoint entirely and
measured semantic coverage of the private cause/remedy after faithful diagnostic
replies. Eight initial LLM hypotheses already covered 9/12 solutions; only one of
three omissions was recovered. Although oracle best-match gain was $.105$ and
5/12 tasks had candidate-dependent spread, the frozen non-saturation and recovery
gates failed. Paprika therefore exhibits path-dependent support changes, but its
short scenarios make the initial semantic differential too saturated for a clean
open-world horizon advantage.

\paragraph{MediQ validates the interface but rejects the joint model.}
MediQ's official FactSelect patient returns only atomic record facts that answer
the question and otherwise abstains \citep{li2024mediq}. After several failed
closed repairs, the final five-case environment gate achieved clean canonical
Yes/No/unavailable questions, verbatim grounding, relevance, and semantic
deduplication on every selected interaction. This isolates the remaining issue
to probability modeling.

The first scorer treated each A--D option as a complete patient world. Across
ten selected turns, predicted EIG averaged $0.140$ nats but realized true-label
log-probability gain averaged $-0.290$ nats; the realized outcome was less likely
under the true label than under the predictive mixture on 6/10 turns. A repair
made record missingness label-independent and scored only Yes versus No under
each option. Missingness became exactly posterior-neutral, yet available
outcomes still had mean true-label log gain $-0.118$ nats.

A final data-estimation construction elicited the response marginal and
hypothetical A--D posteriors, then projected their joint table to exact local
marginals. Before scoring it, a frozen held-out bank required at least 15
available responses. All 30 questions were canonical, unique, grounded, and
relevant, but only 8 were answerable; 22 facts were absent from the static
records. The scorer was therefore not run. At observed answerability $a=8/30$,
an unavailable-neutral binary channel has the loose ceiling
\[
 I(\theta;Y\mid x)\leq a\log 2=0.1848\ \text{nats per query},
\]
before accounting for diagnostic ambiguity. There is little signal on which a
depth-two advantage could operate.

The iCRAFT profile gate failed before likelihood: only 14/48 outcomes were
available versus 24 required. An options-hidden diagnostic raised coverage from
5/20 to 11/20 but failed reliability. On no-leak ClinDiag, full workups raised
diagnosis coverage from 6/20 to 17/20; a five-action follow-up nevertheless had
a non-myopic gap on only 1/12 cases and was unstable. These tests validate
observation-driven belief recovery, not planning.

\paragraph{MovieLens isolates a load-bearing semantic opportunity and a horizon boundary.}
A 12-user recorded-rating gate found that regenerated profiles improved held-out
NLL by $.0612$. One-step semantic ranking passed ($\rho=.274$; 4/4 wins), but
common-tree depth two reversed fidelity ($\rho=-.526$) and worsened NLL
($1.515$ versus $1.484$). Receding rescoring also failed ($1.509$ versus $1.505$
EIG and $1.494$ random), despite changing every first query. Path-dependent
semantic value did not become reliable action selection.

\section{Why Horizon Magnifies Model Error}

Let an estimated continuation score be $\widehat V_j=V_j+\epsilon_j$ for branch
candidate $j$. Depth two uses $\max_j\widehat V_j$, so even zero-mean errors
produce selection bias that grows with width and horizon. Root likelihood error
also changes branch mass and downstream context; more rollouts reduce variance,
not bias \citep{catanach2023misspecification}.
Zendo's entropy scorer had $\rho=0$; external readiness later reached $.80$ on
one rule, but confirmation failed before endpoints. An initial Hotpot audit had
166/500 directional unlocks, yet answer-first beliefs recovered the enabling
document. A fresh train split then had 320/1000 strict unlocks and 31 cases in
which an enabling-first policy improved exact support coverage within the
top-four retrieval roots. The ten-call serving gate passed, but the single
preregistered confirmation stopped after 50 calls when one branch refresh ended
mid-JSON; no continuation scores or policy endpoints were computed, and the run
was not repaired or repeated. MuSiQue passed a 120/120 branching audit and found
the deep child on 2/2 smoke tasks; generic/discrete root fidelity was
$.073/.302$, but neither beat myopic.

BED-LLM's strong one-step results use a concrete entity or profile, small response
space, prior-likelihood construction, and diverse filtered hypotheses
\citep{choudhury2025bedllm}. It finds raw in-context beliefs overconfident and
data-estimation weaker than prior-likelihood BED; our MediQ belief likewise reached
top probability $0.867$ after round one at only 50\% accuracy.

Rock follows LLM-Modulo: the model supplies semantic policies, while an external
component checks grammar, legality, Bayesian updates, and total information gain
\citep{kambhampati2024llmmodulo}. Random-policy and same-root d1 controls attribute
the gain to useful LLM search bias plus external lookahead, not either alone.

UoT reports depth gains on explicit sets of 5 or 15 diseases \citep{hu2024uot}.
CA-BED uses finite hypotheses, soft LLM likelihoods, binary questions, and d2
\citep{arnould2026cabed}, but lacks a causal d1/d2 ablation. In our 12-case
shared-tree Detective audit, d2 score tracked realized entropy reduction
($\rho=.750$) while ranking truth-posterior gain backwards ($\rho=-.208$);
d2 lost $.262$ truth-NLL nats to d1 despite lower final entropy. Thus deeper
semantic planning can amplify likelihood--answerer mismatch into confident-wrong
beliefs even when its nominal information objective behaves as designed.
Replacing numerical introspection by the exact role-conditioned answer function
restored truth-rank correlation to $.933$, but only 23/241 questions changed
between murderer and innocent roles, leaving four rankable cases and two depth
changes. Calibration and non-myopic opportunity therefore traded off rather than
combining into a usable policy.

MuSiQue and BRIGHT lacked root opportunity
\citep{trivedi2022musique,su2025bright}. MultiHop-RAG continuations improved
evidence recall on 2/5 mechanics tasks, but immediate-greedy roots matched the
best lookahead root on all five \citep{tang2024multihoprag}. BrowseComp-Plus had bridges on 75/120
tasks \citep{chen2026browsecompplus}, yet future/full ranking ($.421/.476$)
trailed direct-total ($.571$) and shuffled paths ($.548$); opportunity did not
repair prompted continuation values. Post hoc semantic entropy ranked
evidence/truth at $.857/.847$, but an untouched ten-task gate rejected one
zero-mass belief before endpoints, leaving efficacy unconfirmed.

The $\tau$-Knowledge splits separate scorer fidelity from opportunity. The
first had little oracle-greedy complementarity and a joint scorer discarded much
of its selected root's value. The blind holdout had a larger gap, and
count-dominant receding selection preserved 30/33 available documents in its
first execution, but fresh generation did not reproduce the root advantage.
Better semantic prediction is useful only when candidates expose a real greedy
gap, the next action preserves it, and the generated simulator is stable.

Target-free audits expose the same prerequisite. ClariQ multisample GPT-5.4
changed two of three development roots and gained $+.026$ NDCG, but stability
and holdout-serving gates failed; an open-support replay ranked terminal NDCG
$.207$ versus $.487$ myopic, with shuffling removing the harm. InfoQuest
support refresh lost to fixed support ($.167$ versus $.333$); exact semantic
partitions also lost ($.267$ versus $.433$; 0/7/5 on changed actions). A
checklist audit agreed with 94.7\% of 300 judgments, found variation in 29/30
cells and oracle gain 1.10 versus $.40$, yet dynamic EIG had target correlation
$-.144$ and gain $.30$. A target-need compiler's preregistered linear score
remained anti-aligned ($-.124$, $.433$). Post-hoc max-resolution reached
$.445$ and $.767$, but fresh confirmation reversed: correlation $-.202$ and
gain $.28$ versus $.34$ for both linear and random, with only 2/10 positive
fixtures. The failure is target-misaligned ranking, not absent opportunity or
simulator noise.
DiscoverLLM's released scores are immediate only \citep{kim2026discoverllm}; DAD or RL-BED
amortization cannot repair a broken simulator
\citep{foster2021deep,blau2022rlbed}.

\section{A Validation-First Path Forward}

Future depth studies should cross the following gates in order:
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \setlength{\topsep}{0.25em}
  \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Target and state:} the endpoint decodes $\theta$; latent $z$ generates responses.
  \item \textbf{Prior and support:} held-out log loss beats uniform; truth recall and persistence are measured across regenerated branches.
  \item \textbf{Actions and likelihoods:} roots induce distinct response partitions, repeated identical calls agree, and observations improve true-target log probability.
  \item \textbf{Branch equivalence:} simulation and deployment agree on support, probabilities, and stopping.
  \item \textbf{Structural gap:} an oracle shows d2 beating greedy under native rules.
  \item \textbf{Ranking fidelity:} estimated lookahead ranks oracle value under common candidates and randomness.
  \item \textbf{Policy test:} paired horizons share root candidates, simulator seeds, and stopping.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations and Conclusion}

This is a positive structured-benchmark result and an unstable external
LLM-native result. On $\tau$-Knowledge, one held-out execution passed every
frozen gate with significant ranking and directional endpoints; a preregistered
fresh same-task execution reversed root ranking and tied myopic coverage. The
parser amendment was frozen before holdout access yet followed iterative
development and superseded a no-V4 commitment, so the positive execution was
not a pristine one-shot preregistration.
Rock Diagnosis uses an exact finite simulator and does not test robustness to learned likelihoods or open responses.
Range-gated h3 remains proposal evidence after a tied-control identity mismatch,
so no trajectory ran; h4 is exact-oracle evidence after policy gates failed.
Focused-prior h5 adds exact 500-pair, 15/16 proposal, and 200 positive
trajectories over four seeds, but remains an engineered exact, verifier-routed task.
Mushroom, Cleveland, and Thyroid use exact summaries; the first two use artificial
unlocks and Thyroid uses native delayed-assay metadata plus projected utility.
This is not unaided LLM planning.
Cleveland recovered 100.9 with zero projected branches, but its independent truth-log
interval crosses zero, so it is not an all-gates confirmation. Animals is only partially paired;
its transition confirmation stopped after seven of 60 states on malformed model output,
so it supplies no holdout efficacy estimate.
ClariQ open-support V2 failed serving; its joint-particle replay is post hoc.
Fresh Hotpot remains structural after malformed refresh consumed confirmation;
a fresh asymmetric-prior DiscoverPhysics dark-matter oracle cut MSE 26--39\%
with stable scout selection, but a semantic-likelihood smoke misranked both roots.
Natural location uses one model family and environment. Paprika
policy counts are endpoint-invalid and its structured support saturates at the prior;
MediQ stops before a calibrated policy comparison,
the iCRAFT profile-support gate fails before likelihood, staged iCRAFT recovery
remains below its reliability gate, and ClinDiag has no policy result.
We do not claim that non-myopic BED cannot work.
OpenRouter nondeterminism and failed scorer transfers remain limiting.
The constructive result has two parts. Given a coherent simulator and an
enabling-information gap, LLM proposals plus exact rollout verification produce
a large paired gain. In open retrieval, a receding LLM semantic scorer can
produce significant root and continuation ranking, but the gain is not robust
to fresh generation. Depth still cannot
replace a valid joint model, deployment-matched branches, or genuinely
non-myopic task.

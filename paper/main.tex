\title{When Does Non-Myopic Bayesian Experimental Design Work with Language Models?}
\author{Hanshal Goyal}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Sequential Bayesian experimental design (BED) offers a principled way for
language-model agents to ask questions and plan future information gathering.
Yet deeper planning can optimize errors in an LLM-derived model rather than
useful information. We separate two regimes. In a Number Game with open-ended
executable concepts, Gemini proposes initial and branch-conditioned rule
supports; predictive-risk BED plans over the induced future belief states, and
GPT-5.4 supplies independent target concepts. Across 32 fresh proposal trees,
it lowers two-query Brier loss by 20.5\% versus myopic EIG, 21.4\% versus
fixed-support depth two, 11.6\% versus random, and 10.2\% versus a positive-test
strategy (PTS), with every whole-tree interval excluding zero. The LLM proposal
dynamics are load-bearing. With independent prior draws and fixed policies,
depth three further beats equally cross-fitted depth two by 1.86\%
($[-.0050,-.0012]$), replicated on wholly fresh trees at 2.81\%
($[-.0082,-.0015]$). Finite Rock Diagnosis separately verifies exact
lookahead but leaves the LLM replaceable. DiscoverPhysics preserves a
non-myopic root gain across three trees while regenerated-support value fails
to replicate, localizing a distinct calibration failure. Negative semantic
audits explain why deeper planning otherwise breaks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Interactive agents must often acquire information before committing to an
answer or action. A clinical agent asks about symptoms; a support agent diagnoses
a fault; a guessing agent narrows a large semantic space. Direct prompting is an
unreliable solution: MediQ reports that asking models to interact can reduce
accuracy relative to answering from the initial context \citep{li2024mediq}.
Bayesian experimental design instead selects a query by its expected information
gain (EIG) about a target variable. BED-LLM makes this practical in semantic
spaces by using an LLM to propose hypotheses and provide response likelihoods
\citep{choudhury2025bedllm}.

The natural next step is non-myopic BED: score a query by both its immediate EIG
and the information available to a future adaptive query. Recent methods simulate
conversation trees for this purpose \citep{hu2024uot,arnould2026cabed}. In
principle this can value a prerequisite question with little immediate gain. In
practice every additional branch invokes the same uncertain model, changes the
belief on which later calls condition, and maximizes over more noisy continuation
scores. A larger tree can therefore make a confidently worse decision.

We investigate this distinction using a validation-first sequence. Exact
constrained location and Rock Diagnosis verify the recursion under exact
models. The Number Game then makes the LLM the proposal distribution over an
open-ended executable concept space, extending the support-mismatch setting of
\citet{chari2026wild}. DiscoverPhysics tests branch-conditioned physical
hypotheses under exact simulator likelihoods. $\tau$-Knowledge supplies an
unstructured retrieval contrast with external endpoints \citep{shi2026tau}.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \setlength{\topsep}{0.25em}
  \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item StrategyEIG, an LLM-Modulo sequential BED architecture in which an LLM
  proposes explicit contingent policies and an exact Bayesian component verifies
  their total information gain \citep{kambhampati2024llmmodulo};
  \item preregistered paired evidence that both the LLM proposal prior and
  non-myopic verification are load-bearing in a depth-favorable Rock geometry;
  \item a powered LLM-native result in which non-myopic BED plans over
  branch-conditioned LLM support regeneration and beats myopic, fixed-support,
  random, and PTS controls on independent cross-model target concepts, plus a
  fixed-policy fresh-endpoint result where depth three beats depth two;
  \item a fresh-map confirmation and fresh-tree replication separating robust
  non-myopic root value from generation-unstable LLM support updates;
  \item a cross-environment audit, including fresh-generation retrieval
  instability, that yields a validation contract spanning target sufficiency,
  calibrated likelihoods, branch equivalence, a verified greedy gap, and ranking
  fidelity.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Probabilistic Contract}

Let $h_t=((x_1,y_1),\ldots,(x_t,y_t))$ be the interaction history and
$\theta$ the finite target decoded at the endpoint. A generative state $z$ may
contain additional information needed to predict responses, with
$\theta=g(z)$. The relevant model is
\[
 p_t(\theta,z)\,p(y\mid z,x,h_t), \qquad
 p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)=\sum_z p(y\mid z,x,h_t)p_t(z\mid\theta).
\]
When $\theta$ itself is sufficient, as for a hidden animal answering a property
question, $z$ can be omitted. When $\theta$ is merely a decision label, such as
``treat hypoperfusion first,'' it does not determine a patient's glucose or
history and cannot serve as the response-generating state.

For a coherent model, one-step EIG is
\[
 I_t(x)=\sum_{\theta,y}p_t(\theta)p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)
 \log\frac{p(y\mid\theta,x,h_t)}{\sum_{\theta'}p_t(\theta')
 p(y\mid\theta',x,h_t)}.
\]
Our exact depth-two score is
\[
 Q_2(x)=I_t(x)+\mathbb E_{y\mid x,h_t}
 \left[\max_{x'\in\mathcal X(h_t,x,y)} I_{t+1}(x')\right].
\]
It branches over every positive-mass response, performs a Bayesian update on the
fixed target support, generates branch-specific follow-ups, and adds the best
second-step EIG. This is mathematically appropriate for the model supplied to it.
It cannot determine whether that model describes deployment.

Two requirements are therefore distinct. \emph{Model validity} asks whether the
target, latent state, likelihood, update, and simulated branches form one process
that matches deployment. \emph{Planning value} asks whether the task contains
complementarity or gating such that a lower-immediate-EIG query enables a more
informative continuation. If information has adaptive diminishing returns,
greedy policies can already be competitive \citep{golovin2011adaptive}. Depth
needs a demonstrated gap, not merely a multi-turn interface.

\section{Audit Design and Evidence Rules}

We aggregate only preregistered or reconstructable paired evidence. Exact
location tests use the known source prior and signal likelihood. The animals
audit reconstructs every 20-round binary trial and reports deterministic
bootstrap intervals. Paprika and MediQ use hash-pinned official releases and
strict transcript audits. Failed parsing, unsupported simulator facts, and
endpoint contradictions fail closed.

Most importantly, we distinguish \emph{policy evidence} from \emph{diagnostic
evidence}. A Paprika run whose customer reports the private correct remedy as
failing cannot compare policies, even if aggregate resolution numbers exist.
A MediQ run with an invalid likelihood cannot establish EIG value, even if its
queries are fluent. Such runs may localize a broken link in
Figure~\ref{fig:validation-chain}; they do not support an accuracy claim.

\section{Results Across Environments}

\paragraph{Exact lookahead can work.}
We first constructed a branch-decoy source prior with a local-bump observation
model and movement-constrained queries. The first query can reveal which region
to enter, while movement limits make that choice affect later measurements. Over
120 paired trials, exact depth two reduced final RMSE from $0.5606$ for greedy
EIG to $0.1529$, a mean paired difference of $-0.4077$. This is a harness result,
not an external-benchmark claim. It shows that the implementation and objective
can exploit a genuine delayed-information structure when model error is absent.

\paragraph{One-step semantic BED transfers; depth does not yet.}
On animals 20 Questions, depth-one EIG achieved accuracy AUC $0.743$ versus
$0.465$ for a thinking naive policy and $0.458$ for non-thinking naive over 40
paired trials. The AUC gain over thinking naive was $0.278$ with 95\% bootstrap
interval $[0.191,0.365]$. This is the stable positive BED result.

The apparent aggregate depth-two AUC was $0.770$, but the full depth-one and
depth-two streams were not paired. On the only exact ten-trial seed block,
depth two minus depth one was $-0.040$ with interval $[-0.105,0.025]$ and
3/1/6 wins/ties/losses. Depth three was worse than depth two across 40 paired
trials: $-0.126$ $[-0.224,-0.035]$. Thus these data support semantic EIG, not
non-myopic improvement.

Natural location experiments reinforce this distinction. In a 30-trial
movement-constrained study using exact physical likelihoods but LLM-generated
strategies, final RMSE was $0.412$ for greedy EIG versus $0.929$, $1.127$, and
$0.542$ for horizons 1, 3, and 5. Unconstrained horizons were flat; depth was
non-monotonic and naive remained competitive.

Collaborative Battleship makes the objective issue explicit
\citep{grand2026shoot}. On two independent particle blocks, exact task-utility
lookahead over 25 released LLM-generated question programs raised
three-question next-shot hit probability from $.638/.636$ at h1 to
$.678/.673$ at h3, while greedy configuration EIG reached only $.436/.401$.
But a target-blind GPT-5.4 gate generated 19 safe, balanced behaviors, all
novel to the released bank, and erased the depth effect: receding h1--h3 were
exactly $.591$ and $.580$ on two fresh blocks. Task-aligned h1 still beat
configuration EIG by $.219/.217$. Rich semantic actions can repair utility
alignment without creating non-myopic value.

\paragraph{Planning over LLM proposal dynamics works in the Number Game.}
We use the classic domain $\{0,\ldots,100\}$ and an open-ended space of
executable predicates. Gemini 2.5 Flash proposes 24 safe rules initially and
after both labels of eight candidate queries. Myopic EIG scores the initial
particles. Our depth-two policy instead treats each current particle as a
simulated truth, executes the corresponding regenerated branch, takes its
greedy second query, and minimizes terminal posterior-predictive Brier loss.
Thus the value of a root includes whether the LLM can regenerate a useful
belief state after that answer. GPT-5.4 independently generates target rules;
all models are nonreasoning and targets are hidden from planning. This extends
the documented proposal-support trap in active concept learning
\citep{chari2026wild} with a generator-aware acquisition rule.

In a preregistered powered replication over 32 fresh planning trees and 32
fresh target supports, predictive-risk BED chooses a different root from
myopic and fixed-support depth two on all 32 trees. Mean Brier is $.1836$
versus $.2309$ myopic (20.5\%; whole-tree difference CI
$[-.0628,-.0333]$), $.2335$ fixed depth two (21.4\%), $.2078$ exact random
(11.6\%), and $.2044$ PTS (10.2\%; $[-.0270,-.0143]$). It wins 30/32
trees versus myopic and 27/32 versus PTS. Best-rule Hamming falls 35.1\%
versus myopic and 14.8\% versus PTS; exact-extension coverage also rises.
On extensions absent from planning support, Brier and Hamming gains persist
on 28/32 and 30/32 trees. Two zero-cost provider errors were retried with
identical seeded payloads; 576 successful responses contained no reasoning
tokens or forced exits.

A preregistered role swap made GPT-5.4 Mini the planning generator and Gemini
the independent target generator. Predictive-risk BED again beat PTS
($.1890$ versus $.2267$ Brier; $[-.0532,-.0238]$; 29/32 wins), random by
15.1\%, and myopic by 9.99\% ($[-.0323,-.0111]$). The formal conjunction
failed: the myopic threshold was 10\%, wins were 23/32 rather than 24/32,
Hamming improved 12.8\% rather than 15\%, and three of 512 branches retained
seven valid rules rather than eight. We report this as directional
model-family robustness, not a second all-gates confirmation.

A second fresh 32-tree run with the original model assignment passed every
original gate again: Brier improved 19.7\% over myopic, 21.5\% over fixed
depth two, 11.0\% over random, and 10.4\% over PTS. More importantly, a
preregistered support-rich control pooled all 141--179 generated rules into
one static support per tree. Root-conditioned generation still reduced Brier
from $.2045$ to $.1838$ (10.1\%; $[-.0299,-.0123]$; 22/32 wins) and Hamming
by 17.7\% ($[-.0301,-.0101]$).

A post-hoc exact-size audit removes the remaining count confound. On each of
the 96 independent trees, 32 static supports were sampled from the global rule
pool to match every realized branch size exactly. Root-conditioned generation
reduced Brier 9.93\% ($[-.0240,-.0170]$; 88/96 wins), Hamming 14.61\%
($[-.0209,-.0132]$), and raised coverage 4.01 points; every dataset's
intervals excluded zero, including the model-role swap. A 128-sample
sensitivity gave 9.64\%/14.39\%. This descriptive audit isolates semantic
focusing rather than support count; the preregistered global-pool confirmation
remains the formal mechanism claim.

A zero-call post-hoc ranking audit further checks the simulator's first link.
Across all 96 trees, current-particle terminal risk ranks independent-target
root Brier at mean Spearman $.727$ ($[.675,.774]$) and pairwise concordance
$.806$, while myopic and fixed-depth-two scores are anti-aligned
($-.239/-.237$). The selected root is target-optimal on 45/96 trees and top-two
on 68/96; its mean oracle regret is $.0070$ versus $.0449$ myopic. Mean
leave-one-particle-out root agreement is $.868$, indicating that the ranking
signal is not carried by one indispensable hypothesis.

Generated-only depth three exposed empty twice-conditioned supports. Retaining
consistent parent particles changed 71.0\% of second queries and raised
independent-target exact-support coverage from 22.4\% to 51.8\%. Yet a
preregistered 20-tree test improved Brier only 0.44\%
($[-.0087,.0056]$): retention repaired belief dynamics, but source-to-target
ranking remained moderate (Spearman $.499$) and did not establish a deeper
policy gain.

Cross-fitting with eight independent prior supports raised depth-three rank
correlation to $.771$ versus $.527$ for depth two. The first 32-tree policy
comparison was directional but imprecise; holding its roots fixed, 16 fresh
endpoint draws gave Brier $.1573$ versus $.1602$ (1.86\%;
$[-.0050,-.0012]$), with lower Hamming and higher coverage. A wholly fresh
32-tree replication independently regenerated trees, roots, and endpoints and
again favored depth three: $.1590$ versus $.1636$ (2.81\%;
$[-.0082,-.0015]$), with rank correlation $.917$ versus $.448$. It missed two
auxiliary conjunction gates (one thin support and a 1.81\% rather than 2\%
in-sample-selector gain), which are not repaired.

Pooling both studies post hoc gives a 2.34\% gain
($[-.0059,-.0020]$). A preregistered Qwen endpoint cross-judge also favors the
fixed depth-three policies across 1,024 fresh supports (1.96\%;
$[-.0048,-.0008]$), establishing endpoint-family robustness. Against 416,366
deterministic classical extensions, 50.98\% of unique second-refresh
extensions remain new on every tree, although out-of-bank endpoint mass is too
small for a powered efficacy claim.

\paragraph{LLM-native generated support is not generation-robust.}
In DiscoverPhysics, GPT-5.4 generated eight executable dark-matter maps and
eight branch-conditioned supports; official N-body simulation supplied
likelihoods and held-out trajectories. Immediate EIG chose northeast D while
depth-two risk chose central B. A frozen 5\% regenerated component reduced MSE
from $3.7854$ to $2.9284$ versus D (22.6\%; $[.6393,1.0878]$) and from $3.0006$
versus fixed B (2.41\%; $[.0341,.1100]$). Coverage gain correlated $.279$ with
endpoint gain.

A fresh tree preserved B over D by 13.0\% ($[.2917,.6758]$), but trailed fixed
B by 5.66\% ($[-.2448,-.1050]$) despite 27.8\% better coverage. A fixed-initial
branch isolation again put B over D by 12.4\% ($[.2591,.6094]$), but generated
and fixed B tied ($3.0491$ versus $3.0485$; $[-.0098,.0088]$). A frozen
uncertainty clip made support gains positive on all three endpoints
($.28/.67/.20\%$), but failed preregistered $.5/1\%$ magnitude gates.

\paragraph{LLM-native semantic lookahead is not execution-robust in retrieval.}
Rock's verifier is exact, so we next used the official $\tau$-Knowledge banking
domain: 698 unstructured policy documents, BM25 transitions, and hidden
required-document annotations \citep{shi2026tau}. GPT-5.4 generated
information-need particles, five searches, and document-conditioned follow-ups;
labels were hidden. On a blind 20-task block, full-tree scoring beat myopic
root accuracy ($.7025$ versus $.5537$; $p=.0352$) and retained 30 of 33
available documents versus 26 for myopic receding and 22 for random. Its
future-minus-myopic score ranked exact future gain at $.644$, while belief-only
shuffles were null or adverse.

A preregistered same-task execution regenerated every tree and score in 280
clean calls and reversed root accuracy ($.5575$ versus $.6283$); coverage tied
at 23 and the paired document gain was inconclusive
($p=.3484$, $[-.275,.450]$). A fixed-tree scorer ensemble retained directional
gain but failed its loss cap. The first execution therefore shows semantic
future value and scorer fidelity, not reliable benefit under full regeneration.

\paragraph{Four-hop video retrieval.}
LongVidSearch requires 2--4 necessary clips \citep{yu2026longvidsearch}.
On two disjoint 40-video blocks, a lower-direct-answer root improved four-search
coverage on 10/40 tasks (total gaps 11/10; mean sacrifices $.265/.204$).
GPT-5.4 regenerated distinct grounded supports on all eight synthetic branches,
but ranking stopped on a frozen grammar before paths, scores, or endpoints.
Thus the structural gap and support serving replicate; policy value is unmeasured.

\paragraph{LLM proposals plus exact lookahead work in enabling geometry.}
The negative natural-location sweep did not test the architecture where depth
itself was useful: a later exact width gate showed that its smooth sensing geometry
favored one-step action coverage. We therefore moved to finite Rock Diagnosis.
Movement has exactly zero immediate EIG, but changes distance-dependent sensor
accuracy. The LLM returned six explicit two-action branch policies per decision:
three distinct movement roots followed by checks, and three direct-check roots.
A finite exact posterior and exhaustive observation branching scored every policy;
the LLM was never called inside rollout scoring.

We preregistered 30 paired eight-round trajectories on each of two paper maps,
then scale extensions on the canonical eight-rock RockSample[7,8] geometry from
\citet{smith2004hsvi}, the standard eleven-rock RockSample[11,11] SARSOP instance,
and a frozen fifteen-rock RockSample[15,15] POBAX instance.
This is a diagnosis-only
adaptation: it retains the latent rock vector, grid movement, and distance-dependent
sensor, while excluding sampling, reward, and exit actions.
Controls remove one component at a time: shared d1 scores the identical LLM roots
myopically; width uses exhaustive d1 over all legal actions with matched exact-scorer
units and one LLM ordering call; random strategy uses the same branch grammar and
exact d2 verifier with random legal policies. Table~\ref{tab:rock-strategy} reports
paired gains in mean posterior-entropy AUC; positive is better for StrategyEIG.

The mechanism is direct: StrategyEIG moves to enable accurate checks while both
myopic controls never move. Across the five maps its bounded proposals recover
90.6\%, 86.1\%, 85.8\%, 73.9\%, and 41.1\% of exhaustive d2 value. Beating
shared d1 makes lookahead load-bearing; beating matched random strategies makes
the LLM proposal prior load-bearing. Exact closure of eight-rock roots raised
recovery from 85.8\% to 99.6\%, but optimal-root coverage was 242/270, one state
below the frozen paid-run gate, so it was not tuned or run.

Independent GPT-5.4 Mini and Gemma replications passed entropy and truth-log
controls at eight rocks. Four Gemma seeds and GPT-5.4 Mini did likewise at
11 rocks (2,048 states); a K4 width saturated K6 quality with 9.49 rather than
14.24 scorer nodes per decision. At 15 rocks (32,768 states), three Gemma seeds
pooled gains of .6839, .6815, and .6760 against shared d1, width, and random;
GPT-5.4 Mini independently passed all six gates. K4 used 9.60 nodes per decision
versus 616.94 for exhaustive d2, a registered tree-width rather than wall-clock
comparison.

Proposal capacity remains necessary. Non-thinking E4B recovered only 6.8\% of
exhaustive d2 and failed matched-width and truth-log gates. A non-thinking 12B
model passed all gates across three seeds, recovering 49.5--53.3\%; different
seeds make this a descriptive E4B-to-12B boundary, not a causal size comparison.
Exact verification removes scoring noise but cannot recover a useful branch
absent from the bounded proposal set.

\paragraph{Boundary audits.}
A Gated Sensor control had a large exact d2 gap, but the model proposed a
redundant terminal activation: verification cannot recover setup value absent
from the proposal. Three data-derived unlocks were partial successes. Mushroom
collected 50/50 with zero projected branches, exactly matched d2, and its
truth-log intervals agreed. Cleveland recovered 100.9\% of exact d2 with zero
projected branches, but its independent truth-log interval crossed zero, so it
is not an all-gates confirmation. Thyroid's native delayed-assay metadata
created an exact d2 gap; names exposed ungrounded empirical assay utility, a
utility-card repair failed before endpoints, and a projection-only ablation
collected 0/50 with projected branches. Bounded utility beat d1 and
matched-random continuations, but was not unaided LLM planning.

A range-gated task supplied stronger depth evidence: Exact d3 beat exact d2 by
.4630 and 26B found 16/16 routes, but a tied-control identity audit left no
trajectory, only proposal evidence. Exact d4 gained .3484 (500/0/0);
reasoning-only models stopped, while semantic target selection found 10/10
routes before provider truncation---mechanism evidence, not an LLM-policy claim.
Exact h5 scored 500/500; a compiler found 15/16 routes with .937 recovery. The
initial 50-pair run matched exhaustive d5; three additional preregistered runs
gained .2727 over identical compiled plans (150/0/0) and .2118 over
matched-random h5 (148/2/0). Across four seeds, 51 physical prompts represented
1,200 logical decisions and 200 positive trajectories: an engineered exact
result, not unaided LLM planning.

\paragraph{Natural-task gates.}
Paprika mixes diagnostic questions and world-changing remedies
\citep{tajwar2025curious}, making pure EIG misaligned with resolution utility.
Its simulator also used fixed support and synthetic outcomes while deployment
refreshed support and could terminate; later endpoint review invalidated the
policy counts. A target-hidden check found initial support already covered 9/12
solutions and recovered only one omission, so path dependence was too saturated.

MediQ's FactSelect interface produced grounded Yes/No/unavailable observations
\citep{li2024mediq}, but its joint model failed: predicted EIG was positive while
true-label log probability fell, and only 8/30 held-out questions were
answerable. MediQ therefore stops before a calibrated policy comparison. The
iCRAFT profile-support gate failed before likelihood (14/48 available outcomes);
ClinDiag improved support coverage but had a stable non-myopic gap on only 1/12
cases. MovieLens similarly passed one-step semantic ranking
($\rho=.274$) but reversed at depth two ($\rho=-.526$).

\section{Why Horizon Magnifies Model Error}

For continuation candidate $j$, write
$\widehat V_j=V_j+\epsilon_j$. Depth uses $\max_j\widehat V_j$, so selection
bias grows with width and horizon; root likelihood error also changes branch
mass and downstream context. More rollouts reduce variance, not model bias
\citep{catanach2023misspecification}. This explains repeated failures:
Zendo's scorer had $\rho=0$; Hotpot's unrestricted actions erased its directional
transition; MuSiQue and BRIGHT lacked root opportunity
\citep{trivedi2022musique,su2025bright}; and BrowseComp-Plus future ranking
trailed direct and shuffled controls \citep{chen2026browsecompplus}.

BED-LLM succeeds myopically with concrete entities, small response spaces,
prior--likelihood construction, and filtered hypotheses
\citep{choudhury2025bedllm}. UoT plans over explicit disease sets
\citep{hu2024uot}; CA-BED uses finite hypotheses and soft LLM likelihoods
\citep{arnould2026cabed}. In our Detective audit, d2 tracked entropy reduction
($\rho=.750$) but ranked truth-posterior gain backwards ($\rho=-.208$).
Exact role-conditioned answers restored correlation to $.933$ but removed most
action opportunity. Calibration and a non-myopic gap must coexist.

Rock instead follows LLM-Modulo \citep{kambhampati2024llmmodulo}: the model
proposes semantic policies while an exact component checks grammar, legality,
updates, and information gain. Number Game moves the irreducible semantic
component into the transition itself: future LLM-generated rule support changes
with the simulated answer, while executable predicates retain exact scoring.

Path dependence alone is insufficient. Pi-Bench supports changed on 25/25 tasks
and d2 changed 14/25 roots, yet its fallback equalized second-turn gains.
ClariQ failed stability; InfoQuest's dynamic EIG was anti-aligned; and
$\tau$-Knowledge fresh generation did not reproduce the root advantage.
DiscoverLLM reports immediate scores only \citep{kim2026discoverllm}; DAD or
RL-BED amortization cannot repair a broken simulator
\citep{foster2021deep,blau2022rlbed}. A useful non-myopic policy needs a
structural gap, calibrated branch transitions, and stable semantic generation.

\section{A Validation-First Path Forward}

Future depth studies should cross the following gates in order:
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \setlength{\topsep}{0.25em}
  \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Target and state:} the endpoint decodes $\theta$; latent $z$ generates responses.
  \item \textbf{Prior and support:} held-out log loss beats uniform; truth recall and persistence are measured across regenerated branches.
  \item \textbf{Actions and likelihoods:} roots induce distinct response partitions, repeated identical calls agree, and observations improve true-target log probability.
  \item \textbf{Branch equivalence:} simulation and deployment agree on support, probabilities, and stopping.
  \item \textbf{Structural gap:} an oracle shows d2 beating greedy under native rules.
  \item \textbf{Ranking fidelity:} estimated lookahead ranks oracle value under common candidates and randomness.
  \item \textbf{Policy test:} paired horizons share root candidates, simulator seeds, and stopping.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations and Conclusion}

This remains a positive structured-benchmark result, now with a powered
LLM-native component. In the Number Game, classical BED can evaluate a
fixed rule set, but cannot enumerate the open-ended support or predict how that
support changes after a counterfactual answer. The endpoint remains synthetic,
uses deterministic labels and a restricted safe grammar, and measures two
queries rather than full convergence. One earlier eight-tree replication
failed its preregistered 5\% PTS magnitude gate despite directional gains; the
separately preregistered 32-tree study establishes a powered 10.2\% gain rather
than retroactively rescoring that null.
For three-query planning, the first fresh one-support endpoint comparison also
remains a null. Endpoint precision was selected after that result; the positive
study fixes every root, excludes opened endpoints, and uses 512 fresh responses,
and a wholly fresh planning-tree replication reproduces its primary depth gain.
That replication misses two auxiliary conjunction gates, so broader model and
task robustness remains warranted. A third-family endpoint audit passes, but
planning-generator and task-domain robustness remain open.
An earlier Qwen-planner replication completed all 1,568 planning responses
but failed closed on one truncated generated endpoint. A single fresh
32-tree confirmation instead fixed all 33 canonical Number Game concepts
\citep{tenenbaum2001generalization}: depth three beats depth two by 4.62\%
($[-.0095,-.0010]$) and myopic EIG by 11.37\%
($[-.0190,-.0078]$), with all seven scientific gates and all six diagnostics
favorable. Its registered composite status is nevertheless a null because
two transient provider errors used the bounded retry path; all 1,856 accepted
responses were exact, but the zero-provider-retry gate fails and no rerun is
performed.
An independent fresh 32-tree Qwen replication again passes every scientific
primary against myopic EIG: Brier falls from $.1203$ to $.1051$ (12.61\%;
$[-.0245,-.0070]$; 25/32 wins), with 12.61\% lower Hamming and 1.52 points
higher coverage. Its registered composite also remains a transport null:
nine transparent provider retries exceed the prospectively relaxed cap of
eight by one, despite exact accounting and all support gates. The extra-depth
diagnostic does not replicate in this cohort (0.42\%;
$[-.0043,.0039]$). Thus two independent fresh Qwen cohorts reproduce the
non-myopic-over-myopic effect (11.37\% and 12.61\%), while only one supports a
monotonic depth-three-over-depth-two claim.
A retrospective cohort-stratified synthesis quantifies that robustness:
across 64 disjoint trees, depth three improves Brier over myopic by 12.00\%
($[-.0196,-.0094]$; 50/64 wins), fixed-support depth three by 4.49\%,
PTS by 6.47\%, and random by 8.28\%, with every interval below zero.
This zero-call synthesis cannot rescue either source's transport-null status.
Its depth-three versus depth-two interval still reaches $+.00016$, preserving
the monotonic-depth null.
A second retrospective synthesis adds the two independent GPT-5.4-Mini
blocks. Across four disjoint 32-tree blocks and two planner families, depth
three improves Brier over myopic by 12.48\% ($[-.0192,-.0113]$; 95/128
wins), fixed-support depth three by 5.85\%, PTS by 7.37\%, and random by
8.91\%; every interval is below zero. Qwen and GPT-5.4 Mini separately give
12.00\% and 12.93\%. Pooled Hamming also improves, but depth three versus
depth two remains null ($[-.0042,.0003]$). This model-family synthesis is
retrospective and does not alter source-study statuses.
A four-block first-link audit explains that boundary. Depth three changes the
myopic root on 123/128 trees; simulated-risk advantage predicts exact-endpoint
advantage at Spearman $.423$ ($[.255,.569]$), with mean realized advantage
$.01579$ ($[.01179,.01990]$) and 95/123 wins. Qwen and GPT-5.4 Mini
independently give correlations $.407$ and $.447$, with both family intervals
above zero. Against fixed-support depth three the pooled relationship is
positive but heterogeneous by family; against depth two, mean-advantage and
correlation intervals cross zero. The simulator is calibrated to reject
myopic roots across model families, not universally to order deeper planners.
A separately frozen target-blind stability gate accepts 55/76 depth-three
changes, but yields only 1.30\% over depth two
($[-.00308,+.00015]$) and accepted-change correlation $.107$.
Validation-support stability therefore does not resolve deeper-root ordering.
A prospective 64-tree Qwen confirmation fails during tree 37 before scoring;
its separately frozen 36-tree prefix has first-link Spearman $.617$
($[.367,.773]$) but cannot rescue the run. A fresh two-draw pooled study
lowers Brier 9.56\% versus myopic ($[-.0179,-.0044]$; 23/32 wins), yet margin
calibration is null ($-.037$; $[-.391,.307]$), within-tree SD normalization
does not repair it, and mechanics gates fail.
A preregistered zero-call replay of the 64 fixed GPT-5.4-Mini policies on the
same canonical bank is more heterogeneous: depth three improves only 1.08\%
over depth two ($[-.0045,.0021]$; 19/64 wins), but beats myopic by 12.93\%
($[-.0220,-.0104]$; 45/64 wins). Thus the external bank robustly supports
non-myopic over myopic selection, while the extra-step benefit remains
model- and target-distribution-dependent.

DiscoverPhysics remains a narrow, unstable simulator-grounded LLM-native result.
Its first tree gives 22.6\%
non-myopic and 2.41\% branch-support gains. A fresh structured tree retains the
root gain but reverses the support effect. Thus support value does not replicate across generations,
despite exact rather than learned likelihoods; fixing the
initial tree puts fresh branch support at parity with fixed support.
Rock Diagnosis uses an exact finite simulator and does not test robustness to learned likelihoods
or open responses. Animals is only partially paired.
Natural location uses one model family; Paprika policy counts are
endpoint-invalid; MediQ stops before a calibrated policy comparison; and the
iCRAFT profile-support gate fails before likelihood. Pi-Bench changed 56\% of
roots, but its fallback equalized second-turn gains.
OpenRouter nondeterminism remains a limitation, and depth cannot replace a
coherent simulator or enabling gap. The positive result instead sharpens the
condition: non-myopia helps when it values the future inference process itself,
including the LLM's path-dependent ability to regenerate useful support.
Battleship further shows that a large, novel static experiment bank is not
enough when one-step task-utility replanning can recover the same policy.
We do not claim that non-myopic BED cannot work outside this setting.

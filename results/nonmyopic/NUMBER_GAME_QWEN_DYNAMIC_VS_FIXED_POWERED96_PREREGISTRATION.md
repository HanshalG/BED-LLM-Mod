# Number Game Qwen Dynamic-vs-Fixed Powered-96 Preregistration

Date frozen: 2026-07-30, after the independent confirmation-32 gated null and
before any seed in this experiment is opened.

## Motivation And Power

The fresh 32-tree confirmation favored dynamic support by `4.45%`, but its
paired interval crossed zero and one extreme tree supplied `62.97%` of the
summed gain. An independent retrospective 128-tree audit favored dynamic
support by `5.85%`, with intervals below zero separately for Qwen and
GPT-5.4 Mini planning trees.

Resampling the already reported 32-tree paired differences gives about `60%`
power at 64 trees and `85%` power at 96 trees for the conjunction of a
negative normal-approximation interval and at least `3%` mean improvement.
This experiment therefore uses 96 entirely fresh trees. It is a new powered
replication, not a rerun, seed replacement, or reclassification of the
confirmation-32 result.

## Primary Dynamic-Support Comparison

Compare full path-dependent cross-fit depth three with fixed-initial-support
depth three. Both policies share the initial pooled LLM support, candidate
roots, horizon, validation draws, exact transition logic, and endpoint. Only
the dynamic policy regenerates and retains LLM hypotheses after simulated
observations.

The primary passes only if:

- selected roots differ on at least `48/96` trees;
- dynamic support reduces mean Brier by at least `3%`;
- the paired 95% tree-bootstrap interval for dynamic minus fixed Brier is
  below zero; and
- Brier wins strictly exceed losses. Ties are reported but excluded from this
  breadth comparison because identical roots deterministically produce an
  exact zero treatment contrast.

The wins-minus-losses rule replaces the old raw `16/32` win threshold for this
new experiment. It is frozen before data because the old rule counted
unchanged-root ties as failures even though no support intervention was
deployed. It cannot alter the status of the confirmation-32 result.

## Co-Required Policy Efficacy

Dynamic depth three must also:

- beat myopic EIG by at least `8%` mean Brier;
- have a paired 95% Brier interval below zero; and
- win at least `60/96` trees.

Hamming, exact-target coverage, depth three versus depth two, first-link
calibration, and pooled analyses with prior experiments are diagnostics and
cannot rescue a failed primary.

## Mechanics And Budget

- Exactly `11,040` accepted requests:
  `96 * (2 * 49 Qwen + 1 target + 16 validation)`.
- Exactly `4,704` pooled planning histories, `6,336` total parse events, and
  `11,040` parsed provider draws.
- Exact transport-attempt accounting and at most 288 transparent retries.
- Zero reasoning tokens and forced exits; at most 48 item-salvaged draws.
- Run cost at most `$15.75`; provider-visible starting balance at least
  `$16.50`.
- Every pooled initial support has at least 24 valid extensions.
- Every deployed retained first/second support has at least 12/eight.
- All 16 validation supports per tree have at least 16 valid extensions.

The result is reported once as `passed`, `gated_null`, or `failed_closed`.
There is no continuation, seed replacement, tree exclusion, outlier removal,
third proposal draw, semantic repair, threshold change, support weighting
change, endpoint change, or model swap. The earlier prospective null and
retrospective evidence retain their current labels regardless of this result.

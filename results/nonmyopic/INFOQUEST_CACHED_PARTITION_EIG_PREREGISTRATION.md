## Estimator

For each cached root/world/answer, the other four cached roots are the shared
action bank. Fresh compute-matched GPT-5.4 calls emit:

- dynamic or exact-copy fixed K8 hypotheses;
- positive integer posterior weights `w1..w8`;
- four semantic answer partitions `a1..a8` through `d1..d8`.

The LLM never chooses an action and never sees the official checklist. An exact
deterministic scorer computes answer-cluster entropy in nats and selects maximum
EIG with frozen `A,B,C,D` tie order. Fresh Gemini-2.5-Flash calls answer both
selected continuations from the cached matched histories. Fresh GPT-5.4 Mini
calls score immediate, dynamic-two-turn, and fixed-two-turn official checklist
discovery.

## Serving and Mechanics

Serving uses a hard-coded synthetic initial belief and root answer, then exactly
five calls: dynamic partition, fixed partition, two paired simulator answers,
and one synthetic checklist judgment. Exact five physical requests/HTTP
attempts, all parsers, zero retry/reasoning/forced exits, and cost at most
`$0.12` are required.

Only a serving pass authorizes one mechanics run:

- 30 dynamic semantic-partition calls;
- 30 fixed semantic-partition calls;
- 60 paired follow-up-simulator calls;
- 6 official-checklist calls;

for exactly **126** physical requests and HTTP attempts, capped at `$0.85`.

All batches checkpoint before parsing. No response is repaired, reissued,
coerced, deduplicated, imputed, or dropped.

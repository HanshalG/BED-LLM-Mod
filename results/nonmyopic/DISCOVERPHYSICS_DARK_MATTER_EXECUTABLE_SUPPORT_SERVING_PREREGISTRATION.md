# DiscoverPhysics Dark-Matter Executable-Support Serving Gate

## Status

Frozen before any response under this grammar. This one-call gate tests only
whether GPT-5.4 can emit a diverse, physically compilable open hypothesis
support. Passing authorizes a separately implemented and preregistered
simulator-grounded branch policy. Failure closes this exact grammar without
cleanup, repair, reissue, alternate seed, reasoning, or model swap.

## Motivation

The prompt-only semantic smoke had clean serving and rich path-dependent
supports but misranked both first roots. Its qualitative likelihood selected
southwest rather than the oracle-qualified northeast myopic root, and its
self-scored complete path selected northeast rather than the central scout.

This successor removes the failed component. The LLM remains responsible for
generating a compact open support of semantic hidden-halo maps. A
deterministic compiler makes each hypothesis executable, and any later
likelihood or trajectory score must come from the official simulator.

## Exact Grammar

The model receives only:

- ten concealed positive sources forming one halo;
- region prior NE `.40`, NW `.30`, SW `.20`, SE `.10`;
- coordinate conventions; and
- numeric validity ranges.

It does not receive the oracle map family, source coordinates, likelihoods,
root values, or held-out trajectories.

The response is one exact JSON object containing eight hypotheses. Each has:

- a free-form semantic description and probability;
- region in `NE/NW/SW/SE`;
- continuous center coordinates with region-consistent signs;
- geometry in `compact/radial/tangential/elliptical`;
- continuous major and minor spreads; and
- continuous orientation in degrees.

The deterministic compiler expands compact and elliptical hypotheses on a
ten-point ellipse. Radial and tangential hypotheses become ten-point lines
with a fixed small transverse perturbation. It uses no oracle data.

## Frozen Gates

All conditions are conjunctive:

- exactly one adapter/HTTP request;
- zero reasoning tokens and cost at most `$0.05`;
- strict parse with exactly eight distinct descriptions;
- every region appears and NE has at least two hypotheses;
- at least three geometry types appear;
- summed region-mass L1 error from `[.40,.30,.20,.10]` is at most `.30`;
- every compiled map is finite and remains within coordinate magnitude
  `9.5`; and
- minimum pairwise compiled-map RMS distance is at least `.20`.

No simulator is called and no policy endpoint is accessed in this gate.

## Architecture consequence

The next hypothesis should be coverage repair, not a larger depth sweep or a
generic reasoning escalation. The decomposition sharpens the previous generic
calibration diagnosis: all adverse net h3-h2 contribution lies in the missing-rule
stratum, although this observational partition does not prove adding the true
rule would repair the policy (adding rules also changes weights and actions).

Do not gate refresh solely on an empty posterior. Here h2/h3 never become empty,
yet their missing-rule losses rise. A prospective generative method needs an
explicit open-support predictive model: an initial mixture of independently
proposed executable hypotheses, plus history-conditioned proposals evaluated by
numerical likelihoods. Branch planning must anticipate the SAME refresh operator
used after a real answer, not invent oracle support or choose a hindsight pool.
Shared proposals and canonical deduplication need explicit prior weighting;
frequency weighting and uniform-over-unique rules are different models.

Before spending on policy endpoints, qualify that operator on genuinely fresh,
source-justified tasks against initial-pool-only, equal-call history-blind proposal,
and productive symbolic controls. Freeze predictions before revealed answers and
measure prequential Brier/log loss as well as executable validity. Coverage in a
synthetic/source setting can be an evaluator diagnostic, never a trigger available
to the policy. Full predictive risk includes within-model and between-model
uncertainty. Confirm same-objective horizon opportunity after qualification, with
equal experiment budgets and compute-matched myopic controls. A coverage gain alone
does not establish non-myopic value or LLM necessity.

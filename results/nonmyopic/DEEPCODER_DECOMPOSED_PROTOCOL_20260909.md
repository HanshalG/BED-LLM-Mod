## Arms and allocation

prior restricted to that set. No local mutation, insertion, learned weights, smoothing,
or posterior-completeness claim. Also report the short component alone.

call returns one exact menu choice per prefix and predicted intermediate outputs

before the next call; mismatches are diagnostic, not rejection or retry triggers.

Execution: identical typed prefix construction without predicted subgoals.

Whole: four independent calls to the original whole-program structured schema,
with the same worked examples. Each may return one to eight programs of length 2..4.

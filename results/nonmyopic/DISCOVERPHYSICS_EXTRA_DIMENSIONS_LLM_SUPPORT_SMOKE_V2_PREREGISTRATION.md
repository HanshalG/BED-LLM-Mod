whitespace. Each line has exactly nine pipe-separated fields:

```text

```

Weights are plain integers in `[1,100]`. Parameters are plain decimal numbers,
without exponent notation, units, comments, headers, markdown, or blank lines,
and must satisfy the unchanged V1 bounds.

before parsing. The parser performs no cleanup, coercion, deduplication, or

# Number Game External Canonical-Target Replay Result

## Decision

**Composite gated null, with a clean preregistered non-myopic-versus-myopic
component result.**

The zero-call replay evaluated all 31 complete frozen Qwen planning trees on
all 33 concepts listed by Tenenbaum and Griffiths (2001). Every source hash,
target-count, uniqueness, and endpoint-construction check passed. The complete
conjunction did not.

Candidate-minus-baseline differences are shown. Lower Brier is better.

Depth three improves on depth two by a substantial 5.70%, but the upper
confidence limit is positive by 0.000140. It therefore fails the frozen
strict-below-zero gate. Depth three cleanly beats myopic EIG and the random
root control on this external bank. These are preregistered component results,
but they do not override the failed all-gates conjunction.

Hamming improves by 2.15% versus depth two, while exact truth-extension
coverage falls by 0.59 percentage points. Rank fidelity is weak on the
external bank: mean depth-three Spearman is 0.265 versus 0.157 for depth two,
well below the frozen 0.7 and +0.15 gates.

## Interpretation

This addresses an important endpoint-provenance concern. The frozen LLM
belief trees produce a robust non-myopic-over-myopic Brier gain on concepts
specified by the cognitive-science literature rather than generated by an
endpoint LLM. Evidence for the additional depth-three-over-depth-two step is
encouraging but not conventionally resolved on this 31-tree bank.

The result does not create a fresh planner-family replication: the Qwen trees
were previously opened, and only this external endpoint was new. The paper's
33 concepts were defined on integers 1--100; the preregistration fixed their
natural predicate extension to this project's 0--100 domain before scoring.

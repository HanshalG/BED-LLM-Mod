- all `4/4` roots produced different supports under their two outcomes;
- all eight continuations differed from their roots; and
- the final scorer had a unique, varying maximum.

time-dependent and time-invariant alternatives with targeted phase

truth-coverage endpoint. Because the frozen margins failed and no simulator
was called, this cannot establish improved experiment selection or scientific
discovery.

# DiscoverPhysics Balanced Modular Replication

## Status

Frozen before any new model response. This is a distinct support-generating
process, not a rerun or threshold repair of the closed structured V3,
conservative-cap, or modular-cut protocols.

## Fixed Motivation

The structured fresh tree reproduced the non-myopic B-versus-D gain but its
branch-generated support lost `5.66%` to same-root fixed B. One center branch
assigned `95.2%` of generated mass to NE and only `3.8%` to NW. Exact posterior
decomposition showed the data-dependent component was over-promoted.

Cutting component feedback improved same-root fixed B on both open trees, but
the original-tree gain (`0.9595%`) missed a frozen `1%` development gate. That
exact method is closed.

This successor changes the generator, not the old threshold:

1. every branch must preserve broad regional support; and
2. component-level feedback remains cut because support is generated after the
   root observation.

## Fresh Support Process

Use `openai/gpt-5.4`, reasoning disabled, requested temperature `0`, and the
proven strict structured-output route.

Request exactly:

- one discarded strict initial-support preflight;
- one scientific initial eight-map support; and
- eight scientific branch refreshes, two per root.

Every refresh must return exactly eight valid maps with:

- exactly two hypotheses in each of NE, NW, SW, and SE;
- at least three geometry types;
- a continuation different from its root; and
- positive integer within-region weights.

After strict parsing and compilation, deterministically project each refresh's
regional mass to the disclosed prior NE/NW/SW/SE = `.4/.3/.2/.1`, preserving
the LLM's relative weights within each region. The LLM still owns descriptions,
centers, geometry, spread, orientation, within-region weighting, and branch
continuation. No hypothesis is added, deleted, repaired, or moved between
regions.

The initial-support prompt, schema, parser, compiler, action bank, and branches
remain unchanged.

## Modular Policy

For every realized root/continuation history, compute exact conditional
posteriors separately inside initial and refreshed support. Predict with:

```text
0.95 * initial_component_prediction
+ 0.05 * refreshed_component_prediction
```

Component identity is not updated by likelihood. There is no cap, grid,
temperature, region-specific mixture, or endpoint-fitted scalar.

## Phase A Gates

All must pass before endpoint access:

- exactly 10 accepted responses;
- `http_attempts == requests + retry_count`;
- zero reasoning tokens and zero forced exits/finals;
- cost at most `$0.25`;
- all ten responses pass strict schema and semantic compilation;
- all eight refreshes differ from the scientific initial support;
- at least three roots have branch-distinct supports;
- center B branches choose distinct continuations;
- all eight refreshes have exactly two hypotheses per region;
- all projected regional masses equal `.4/.3/.2/.1` within `1e-12`;
- every refresh has at least three geometry types;
- immediate EIG selects D;
- modular-cut trajectory risk selects B; and
- B reduces modular-cut internal trajectory risk by at least `10%` versus D.

Failure closes the method before the physical endpoint. No second tree,
ordinary-JSON fallback, support coercion, or threshold change is allowed.

## Scientific Gates

All must pass:

- B reduces fresh-map MSE by at least `10%` versus D;
- paired interval lower bound for `MSE(D)-MSE(B)` is positive;
- B reduces MSE by at least `5%` versus random A;
- modular B reduces MSE by at least `1%` versus fixed B;
- paired interval lower bound for `MSE(fixed B)-MSE(B)` is positive; and
- routed retained-union nearest-support risk improves by at least `5%`.

Any failure is a replication null. Do not inspect alternate region weights,
component masses, map subsets, or additional trees.

## Claim Scope

A pass would show that an LLM-generated, branch-conditioned hypothesis
expansion adds repeatable value when its semantic breadth and module feedback
are controlled prospectively. It would remain one physical domain with a
deterministic compiler and exact likelihoods.

## Integrity and limits

43 focused tests pass (2.11s), including an independent uniform-bit calculation,
menu restriction with unqueried targets retained, full-control plateau behavior,
and the preceding interpreter/belief/search tests. Exact saved row-to-aggregate
arithmetic was checked independently after the run. Source bindings passed before
execution. Reuses the earlier exact membership and open-loop planners, without
altering their banked code or results.

The uniform finite-function prior and one/two-object universe are explicitly
constructed reference assumptions, not a measured prior for human concepts or LLM
proposals. This is not a source-grounded visual ZendoWorld result. The symbolic
grammar is fully enumerated here to establish reference opportunity; the work-limited
real-history search and LLM proposer have NOT been compared on fresh tasks. Those
claims cannot be inferred from this oracle panel.

The positive but small gaps suggest immediate information captures most of the
available gain under these settings. They do not show all semantic induction is
myopic or rule out measurements that improve a bounded future discovery algorithm.
That stronger mechanism would require its own prospective, evidence-grounded
transition model, rather than treating this small static grammar as LLM-native.

## Decision

Close this exact configuration. No new menus, selected rule subsets, budget changes
or weakened thresholds to rescue it; no LLM spending on this panel. Retain reusable
interpreter/search code, but do not extend this small synthetic route into another
large mechanics project. The primary unresolved problem remains useful,
evidence-responsive model discovery and a credible planning opportunity in the
same task, not a shortage of planner implementations.

The full research goal remains active and unachieved. This null changes the next
action: the planned paid-proposal path through this reference gate does not open.

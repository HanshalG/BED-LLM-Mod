# Bongard OpenWorld Path-Mediation Paper Handoff Amendment

Frozen: 2026-08-09, before any August 10 Bongard model response, candidate
label, endpoint label, or terminal artifact was opened.

Status: **prospective zero-call reporting amendment; authorizes no model
request**.

## Problem

The prospective path-mediation audit independently replays the central
belief-to-action-to-endpoint chain: answer-conditioned versus same-seed
history-blind intermediate LLM beliefs after the same first query and answer,
their second-query score maps and actions, and endpoint effects under a common
terminal updater. The existing mandatory paper wrapper can still render a
development or confirmation result without requiring or reporting that
artifact. That permits the most direct diagnostic of the LLM-native mechanism
to be omitted after outcomes are known.

## Mandatory Handoff

Paper-wrapper V5 must require, independently replay, and bind the exact frozen
path-mediation report whenever a development or confirmation endpoint result is
rendered. It must reject a missing report, authorization failure, stage or
stage-result mismatch, changed protocol or implementation, malformed or
non-finite summary, wrong task count, inconsistent action counts, or malformed
all-task paired endpoint summary.

The generated body must report, for all tasks in the rendered stage:

- mean canonical-rule Jaccard overlap;
- mean candidate and endpoint predictive-probability differences;
- mean second-query score-rank correlation;
- changed, robustly changed, and mutually robust second-action counts;
- the all-task dynamic-minus-history-blind-matched endpoint Brier effect and
  paired 95% interval; and
- the two registered descriptive associations with realized Brier benefit.

The paragraph is mandatory regardless of metric direction. Selected changed or
robust subsets remain available in the bound JSON but cannot replace the
all-task effect.

## Supersession

Paper-wrapper V5 replaces V4 as the mandatory final development and
confirmation rendering path. It preserves the V4 Luna fragment, DINOv2 and
SigLIP2 suite, compute-matched and random audits, classical headline scope, and
all result-tier mappings, while adding the required mediation replay and body
paragraph. Direct publication from V4 is scientifically incomplete after this
amendment.

## Claim Boundary

This is descriptive mechanism reporting, not a standalone causal mediation
estimate. Causal interpretation remains limited to the prospectively matched
dynamic-first intermediate-updater policy contrast and its all-task endpoint
gate. The report cannot alter, rescue, or veto any scientific gate, claim tier,
confirmation authorization, classical scope, headline, or paid authorization.

This amendment changes no model, reasoning mode, prompt, image, task, split,
seed, candidate, endpoint, policy, likelihood, action, updater, request count,
cost cap, or result mapping. The wrapper and audit make zero model calls, cost
`$0`, and authorize no paid call, rerun, repair, descendant, endpoint opening,
or claim. A mechanics failure has no paper endpoint result and must reject a
mediation artifact if one is supplied to the paper wrapper.

isotropic Gaussian noise of standard deviation `.075`.

## Policies And Endpoint

The uniform prior covers all 24 maps.

- Myopic chooses the root with maximum one-step mutual information.
- Depth two chooses the root with maximum total mutual information after an
  observation-conditioned best continuation from the same action bank.

Both use the exact same Gaussian likelihood and common action bank. Monte
Carlo decision estimates use seed `24494`, 12 root samples per hypothesis,
and four continuation samples per hypothesis. An independent confirmation
uses seed `24495` with the same counts.

The behavioral endpoint is posterior-predictive mean squared position error
on two frozen official N-body held-out experiments, each containing five
active probes and six measurement times through `t=5`. This is the same
probe-trajectory quantity used by the official dark-matter evaluator.

## Frozen Gates

All conditions are conjunctive:

- myopic and depth-two roots differ in both decision and confirmation;
- both selected roots replicate exactly across the two Monte Carlo seeds;
- depth two sacrifices at least `.03` nats of immediate information;
- depth two gains at least `.03` nats of total two-step information over the
  myopic-root policy; and
- depth two reduces held-out trajectory MSE by at least `10%` relative to the
  myopic-root policy in the independent confirmation.

The root policy always gets an adaptive second experiment. The comparison is
therefore about the first design's enabling value, not one versus two calls.

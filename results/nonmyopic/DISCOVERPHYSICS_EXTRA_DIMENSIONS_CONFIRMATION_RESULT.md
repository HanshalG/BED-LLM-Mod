# DiscoverPhysics Extra-Dimensions Structural Confirmation Result

## Decision

All eight frozen gates passed. The official-simulator result establishes a
stable scout-then-target opportunity and authorizes only a separately
preregistered, capped LLM executable-support smoke.

This is not yet an LLM-policy result. The support used here is the frozen
18-member oracle instrument.

## Official Result

At the primary 24-point Gauss-Hermite evaluation:

| Metric | Myopic | Adaptive depth two |
|---|---:|---:|
| Selected radius | `5.5` | `2.4` |
| Immediate EIG | `1.806560` | `1.605525` |
| Total depth-two EIG | `2.333566` | `2.436034` |
| Held-out prediction MSE | `.00385408` | `.00215911` |

The adaptive scout therefore:

- gives up `.201035` immediate nats;
- gains `.102468` total EIG nats; and
- reduces held-out log-force prediction MSE by `43.98%`.

The 16- and 32-point evaluations return the same roots. Their total-EIG gains
are `.102508` and `.102675`; risk reductions are `44.38%` and `44.17%`.

## Mechanics

All 72 official action-hypothesis displacements are finite and positive. A
separate NumPy implementation of the frozen Yoshida integrator agrees with
the official pinned `NBodySampler` to maximum absolute displacement error
`4.440892098500626e-16`, far inside the frozen `1e-7` tolerance.

Every frozen gate is `true`:

- exact myopic and adaptive root IDs;
- minimum immediate sacrifice;
- minimum total-EIG gain;
- minimum predictive-risk reduction;
- quadrature stability; and
- independent-integrator agreement.

## Scope

This result proves the environment has an adaptive opportunity under a broad
prior over official Kaluza-Klein, Yukawa, and Riesz kernels. It does not show
that an LLM can generate adequate support, preserve useful hypotheses after a
branch, rank the scout correctly, or improve a realized endpoint.

The next stage must keep the numerical actions and likelihood computation
machine-owned. The LLM may generate only executable initial and
branch-conditioned supports. Reasoning remains reserved for a naive baseline.

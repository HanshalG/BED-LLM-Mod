# Number Game Qwen Cross-Judge Depth-Three Preregistration

Date frozen: 2026-07-28, before any Qwen 3.7 response.

## Question

Does the cross-fitted depth-three advantage survive when its fixed policies
are evaluated against target concepts generated by a third model family?

The two source studies both used GPT-5.4 Mini to generate their planning
trees and Gemini 2.5 Flash for independent validation and endpoint concepts.
This study holds all 64 selected roots and planning trees fixed, then replaces
only the endpoint concept generator with `qwen/qwen3.7-plus`.

- fixed-policy fresh-endpoint source `RESULT.json`

  and `TREES.json`

  and `TREES.json`
  `197dcfe3cb48eeb9a4b656d0f9b20ef2e0b45ac8d1df0ec4bd9358e07af18802`.

No Qwen endpoint can alter a root, support transition, risk score, candidate
set, or policy. The source studies retain their original statuses.

## Serving Gate

Before a scientific endpoint, issue exactly 10 generic prior-support requests
at seeds 31610--31619. Use the unchanged Number Game prompt, strict schema,
parser, temperature 0.7, and 4,096-token output cap. Qwen reasoning is
explicitly disabled.

The gate passes only if:

- all 10 accepted responses parse to at least 16 valid unique rule
  extensions;
- accepted request and HTTP/retry accounting is exact;
- retries are at most eight;
- reasoning tokens and forced exits are zero; and
- cost is at most `$0.10`.

The smoke does not score or inspect any scientific endpoint. Failure closes
this exact Qwen route without parser, prompt, seed, model, or threshold repair.

## Conditional Formal Study

Only after the serving gate passes, generate 16 independent Qwen target
supports for each of the 64 fixed trees. Seeds are consecutive from 31700,
grouped deterministically by study, tree, and draw. This is exactly 1,024
accepted requests.

Supports are equally weighted within each tree; trees are equally weighted.
Inference, branch histories, policy execution, deterministic labels, Brier,
best-rule Hamming, exact-extension coverage, novel-target definitions,
controls, and 50,000-replicate whole-tree bootstrap are unchanged.

Primary comparison: cross-fitted depth three versus equally cross-fitted
depth two.

The formal result passes only if every gate holds:

- exactly 1,024 accepted requests, exact attempt accounting, at most eight
  retries, zero reasoning tokens, zero forced exits, and cost at most `$3.50`;
- every tree has 16 draws, every draw has at least 16 valid rules, and every
  tree has at least 128 target hypotheses novel to its initial support;
- the already-fixed policies differ on exactly 35/64 roots;
- pooled depth-three Brier improves at least 1%, its whole-tree 95% interval
  is strictly below zero, and it wins at least 23 trees;
- each source study is directionally positive on mean Brier;
- pooled Hamming and coverage do not regress;
- novel-target Brier, Hamming, and coverage do not regress;
- depth three beats myopic EIG, fixed-support depth three, and PTS by at
  least 3% Brier, with each whole-tree interval strictly below zero; and
- depth-three source-to-endpoint Brier Spearman correlation is at least 0.7
  and exceeds depth two by at least 0.15.

There is one run, no seed replacement, failed-response removal, threshold
repair, selective source pooling, or root reselection. A failed conjunction
is reported as a gated null while all measured components remain public.

## Budget And Interpretation

The formal stage requires at least `$3.00` live credit and has a hard `$3.50`
run cap. Concurrency is 128. Both stages use OpenRouter only.

A pass establishes target-model-family robustness for the fixed LLM-native
depth-three policies. It does not establish a third planning-generator family
and is not a third independent policy-selection replication.

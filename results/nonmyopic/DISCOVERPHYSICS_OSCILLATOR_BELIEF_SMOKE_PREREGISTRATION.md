## Conceptual Action Compiler

The LLM chooses an experiment using exactly five semantic factors:

- separation: `near`, `medium`, or `far`;
- initial motion: `rest`, `tangential_slow`, or `radial_outward`;
- absolute start phase: `phase_0`, `phase_1`, `phase_2`, or `phase_3`;
- source strength: `low` or `high`; and
- probe inertia: `light` or `heavy`.

The target-blind deterministic compiler maps these to separations
`.75/2/5`, velocities `[0,0]/[0,.25]/[.25,0]`, absolute start times
`0/1/2/3`, and scalar properties `.5/2`. Every experiment receives the fixed
ten-point relative-time schedule `.5,1,...,5`.

The compiler removes only numerical schedule serialization. It does not
select an action, expose a likelihood, reveal the world, score a hypothesis,
or use an outcome. The 144-action conceptual space is fixed before responses.

## Exact Ten-Call Plan

All calls are non-reasoning, temperature zero, exact JSON, and fail closed.

1. One initial-tree call generates five distinct semantic law hypotheses and
   probabilities, four distinct conceptual root experiments, and two
   mutually exclusive qualitative outcome branches per root. Every branch
   includes its probability and a posterior over the same initial support.
2. Eight isolated refresh calls, one for each root/outcome pair, receive only
   the apparatus, current belief, that root, that hypothetical outcome, and
   its old-support posterior. Each regenerates a fresh five-law support and
   probabilities, estimates the probability that the true law is adequately
   represented by that support, and chooses one different continuation.
3. One blinded scorer call receives the four complete branch-conditioned
   pathways as A-D, but no immediate EIG. It returns four integer final-law
   readiness scores from 0 to 100.

There is no retry for semantic or formatting failure beyond transport retries
already provided by the shared OpenRouter adapter. Raw responses are
checkpointed after every phase.

## Policies And Signals

Exact code computes fixed-support immediate EIG from the initial branch
probabilities and old-support posteriors. The myopic root maximizes this value,
with frozen root order breaking ties.

The model-aware root maximizes the final-law readiness score from the tenth
call. Its preregistered mediator is expected regenerated-support coverage:
the initial model's branch probability times the isolated refresh call's
coverage probability, summed over the two branches.

This is intentionally an open-world causal signal rather than entropy over
unrelated support identities. The LLM is load-bearing for:

- semantic law-space generation;
- qualitative likelihood and posterior prediction;
- branch-conditioned support regeneration;
- estimating whether the regenerated support covers the unknown law;
- continuation experiment design; and
- ranking complete future belief pathways.

The released hidden law and simulator never enter these calculations.

## Frozen Gates

All mechanics must pass:

- exactly 10 logical/adapter requests;
- zero reasoning tokens;
- cost at most `$0.50`;
- all exact schemas parse with no trailing content;
- every root and continuation compiles to a legal ten-time experiment;
- at least 6/8 refreshed supports differ from the initial support;
- at least 3/4 roots have distinct refreshed supports across their two
  predicted outcomes;
- expected coverage varies by at least `.05` across roots;
- root scores vary and have one unique maximum.

All scientific mechanism criteria must also pass:

- model-aware and myopic roots differ;
- the model-aware root gives up at least `.01` nats of immediate EIG;
- its expected regenerated-support coverage is at least `.05` higher than
  the myopic root's; and
- its final-law readiness score is at least five points higher.

These criteria are conjunctive. A pass is evidence that the proposed
LLM-native signal exists and is mechanically actionable, not that the policy
improves scientific discovery.

## Conditional Paired Experiment

controls are:

- fixed-support myopic EIG;

All execute under common random numbers and the same two-experiment budget.
External endpoints must include official executable-law trajectory error,
concept identification, and whether the true law is represented in the
realized regenerated support. The paired experiment gets a separate cost cap
and cannot use smoke responses as policy evidence.

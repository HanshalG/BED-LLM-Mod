# RegretBench Option-ID Codec Source Protocol

Frozen: 2026-08-11 Europe/London, after the proposal-evaluator-v1 terminal reply-codec null and before any option-ID model response.

## Purpose

Freeze a completely new RegretBench cohort for an executable option-ID reply interface. Exclude the union of all 132 tasks in the original LLM-native source manifest and all 132 tasks in the factorized-v2 source manifest. This excludes every task used by either closed Aug 11 smoke as well as their unopened development and confirmation cohorts.

From the remaining eligible official OpenDomainQA test tasks, hash-rank with salt `regretbench-option-id-codec-v1|` and freeze, in order:

- `codec_calibration`: 2 tasks;
- `mechanics`: 2 tasks;
- `development`: 64 tasks; and
- `confirmation`: 64 tasks.

All 132 selected tasks must be disjoint from the 264 prior tasks, mutually disjoint, prompt-unique, answer-alias nonleaking, checksum-valid, and have exact zero fixed-support depth-two gain under the established source audit. Codec calibration responses may never select, tune, replace, or reveal the mechanics tasks.

Passing the source audit authorizes only a separately frozen option-ID codec serving gate on the two codec-calibration tasks. It authorizes no mechanics call, endpoint, development, confirmation, or paper claim.

## Visibility

Proposal and likelihood-evaluator calls may see only public task identity, public ambiguous prompt, and model-generated objects. Environment codec calls may additionally see the selected public clarification question, its model-generated option labels, and indexed unique values of the selected source facet. They may not see intent descriptions, intent probabilities, final answers, answer aliases, hidden truth, task endpoints, candidate policy scores, or development/confirmation records.

Source values may be loaded only after the proposal/evaluator has selected a question without them. Their sole role is to test whether the environment can execute that public question into the frozen option-ID alphabet.

## Purpose

Define a genuinely new RegretBench belief interface on tasks that were frozen before, but not sent during, the factorized-v2 smoke. This protocol does not relax or reinterpret the closed factorized-v2 transition gate.

The predecessor source manifest froze four ordered mechanics tasks. The exact factorized-v2 producer loaded only positions zero and one. Its banked prompt-privacy hashes are replayed against all four root payloads. Proposal-evaluator v1 may use only positions two and three if and only if:

- both paid root hashes equal positions zero and one in order;
- neither remaining root hash appears anywhere in the six paid prompt audits;
- the factorized-v2 terminal status is `failed_closed`, accepted exactly two roots and four transitions, issued zero static calls, and authorizes nothing;
- the original source audit, task files, terminal report, public terminal audit, failure, stage ledger, and public hash-only projection of the private raw/privacy banks match their frozen hashes; the projection must bind the private hashes, exact six payload hashes/roles, and raw response keys without exposing prompts or responses; and
- no factorized-v2 result, verification, endpoint, development, or confirmation artifact exists.

Passing this audit authorizes only a separately frozen proposal-evaluator mechanics smoke on the untouched pair. It does not authorize model calls by itself, endpoint access, development, confirmation, efficacy analysis, or a paper claim.

## Scientific Boundary

The successor must replace the failed transition contract rather than repair it in place:

- no retain/revise labels, parent lineage claims, or forced retention quota;
- support proposals emit no prior weights or likelihood predictions;
- answer-conditioned and answer-free proposals share seeds but use distinct frozen prompts;
- a separate evaluator sees the original public prompt, fixed proposed hypotheses, and indexed questions, but no dialogue or dedicated observed/simulated-reply field; generated proposal text may itself reflect the conditioning history;
- the evaluator assigns history-free prior weights and predicted replies;
- code applies each branch reply exactly once to both conditioned and answer-free supports; and
- the answer-free compute control never has to invent unsupported semantic revisions.

Any later policy endpoint remains sealed behind a distinct development preregistration and a passed mechanics interface.

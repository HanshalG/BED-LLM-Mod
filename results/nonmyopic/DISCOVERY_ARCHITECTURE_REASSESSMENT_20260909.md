## New primary-source evidence

[Large Discovery Models, Aug16 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.15669v1), sections2-3,
separates proposal reachability from uncertainty within the surrogate's support.
Its architecture couples context-dependent structured generation, a probabilistic
reward surrogate, and acquisition-guided search. This motivates separating

optimization framework; the inspected formulation does not establish our terminal
predictive BED objective or ordinary horizon1/2/3 monotonicity. More inference-time
search is not itself deeper experimental planning. Do not cite its reported
optimization gains as evidence that our belief-transition model is calibrated.

## First-principles correction

The accumulated evidence identifies two different obstacles:

1. The DeepCoder LLM failed joint predictive coverage even with reasoning and
   execution feedback. More sophisticated planning on that belief is unsupported.
2. The string source prior admitted calibrated internal numerical calculations but
   offered no measured ordinary-depth separation across the fixed12 development
   tasks. Better semantic proposals alone do not create a demonstrated planning gap.

Neither adding a bigger hypothesis list nor selecting the highest rollout entropy
drop addresses both. The decision-relevant state must include public history and
the actual proposal memory/support, and the transition must represent the deployed
updater rather than simply condition a frozen hypothesis list.

Candidate architecture for a genuinely new interface:

- A source-defined executable world generates linked experimental answers and
  target outcomes. The planner uses an independently qualified predictive world
  distribution, not probabilities aligned onto newly added zero-mass hypotheses.
- A semantic LLM proposes typed, executable mechanism edits conditioned on public
  residuals, observations and previous rejected proposals. Code owns execution,
  evidence weighting, numerical updates and proper predictive loss.
- Each hypothetical answer invokes exactly the same updater interface used after
  a real answer. Start with a short explicit tree; amortize transitions only after
  held-out atlas-versus-live fidelity, including rare informative answers, passes.
- Score the resulting predictor under an independently qualified joint world law.
  Do not equate its self-entropy with expected realized accuracy. Track unsupported
  branches explicitly; silently conditioning them away is not Bayesian evaluation.
- Include real-history-only refresh, fixed-support planning, matched-compute myopic
  refresh and random-query controls. An LLM-free symbolic search baseline is needed
  to measure whether the semantic proposer contributes beyond numerical planning.

These are design requirements, not a newly validated method. Much of this matches
the earlier M-open direction, but its old failed interfaces remain closed. Any new
version needs an explicit change in observation/model contract and a fresh test.

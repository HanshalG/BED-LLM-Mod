# ICAE Selective Full-World Instrument Smoke

Date: 2026-07-29

## Purpose

Test a distinct ICAE belief representation after the atomic-hypothesis
instrument saturated its union endpoint. Each particle is now one competing
latent implementation-contract world containing six jointly asserted,
falsifiable clauses. Probabilities sum to one across eight worlds. Hidden
coverage is measured separately within each world and then averaged using its
probability; clauses from different worlds are never unioned.

This is a development smoke on the already-open mechanics partition. It does
not reopen or tune the failed Ruby task, and it does not authorize a policy
claim.

## Exact Ten Calls

1. eight initial worlds and six discriminating questions;
2. six hypothetical positive answers;
3. complete 8-by-6 semantic likelihood matrix;
4. first-question positive-branch world refresh;
5. first-question fallback-branch world refresh;
6. semantic retention of each initial world in each branch;
7. realized set-valued trigger match;
8. realized-history world refresh;
9. per-world hidden-requirement coverage endpoint;
10. exact repeat of the endpoint.

Every raw response is privately checkpointed before parsing. No repair,
reissue, retry, task/model/seed substitution, development-or-later partition,
or executable test endpoint is allowed.

## Gates

All must pass:

- exact 10 accepted requests and HTTP attempts;
- zero retries, provider retries, reasoning tokens, and forced exits;
- all schemas, orderings, and cardinalities parse exactly;
- each support has eight unique worlds, each with six unique clauses,
  positive probabilities summing to one, and effective world count at least
  `2.0`;
- at least three distinct world-question likelihood values;
- both simulated branches change at least four of six questions and produce
  different world supports;
- positive/fallback initial-world retention counts differ by at least one;
- the realized root matches at least one released trigger;
- realized history changes at least four questions and the world support;
- duplicate endpoint judgments are exact;
- probability-weighted hidden coverage is in `[0.20, 0.90]`;
- maximum minus minimum per-world hidden coverage is at least `0.10`; and
- cost is at most `$0.50`.

Passage authorizes only a separately frozen full-world paired measurement on
an unopened cohort. Failure closes this exact full-world instrument.

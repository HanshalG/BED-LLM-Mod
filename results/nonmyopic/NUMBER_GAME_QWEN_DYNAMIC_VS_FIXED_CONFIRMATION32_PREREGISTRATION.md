## Primary Dynamic-Support Comparison

Compare full path-dependent cross-fit depth three with fixed-support depth
three. Candidate and baseline share the initial pooled LLM support, candidate
roots, horizon, validation draws, exact transition logic, and endpoint. The
candidate alone regenerates and retains support after simulated observations.

The primary passes only if:

- selected roots differ on at least `24/32` trees;
- dynamic support reduces Brier by at least `3%`;
- the paired tree-bootstrap Brier-difference interval is below zero;
- dynamic support wins at least `16/32` trees.

## Required Policy Efficacy

Dynamic depth three must also beat myopic EIG by at least `8%` Brier, have a
paired Brier interval below zero, and win at least `20/32` trees.

Hamming, exact-target coverage, depth three versus depth two, first-link
calibration, and second-refresh-only comparisons are diagnostics and cannot
rescue a failed primary.

# RegretBench Refresh-Matched Myopic Amendment

Date: 2026-08-07

Status: frozen before any RegretBench policy response or endpoint.

## Problem

The `myopic_brier` amendment matches dynamic depth two's truth-group Brier
utility, but its one-step posterior is computed by conditioning the fixed
initial support. Dynamic depth two instead evaluates supports regenerated by
the LLM after a simulated first answer. Therefore dynamic versus
`myopic_brier` can still combine a horizon effect with selecting questions
that induce better immediate LLM regeneration.

## Refresh-Matched Control

Add `myopic_refresh_brier` as a primary policy. For every candidate first
question, every initial generated hypothesis treated as truth, and both
already frozen conditioned branch draws:

1. use the exact conditioned support generated after that hypothesis's
   aligned predicted first reply;
2. sum its normalized probability on regenerated hypotheses whose final
   answer matches the treated truth under the frozen lexical matcher;
3. score immediate `(1 - truth-group mass)^2` and frozen-floor log loss; and
4. average over draws and treated truths using normalized initial priors.

`myopic_refresh_brier` selects the minimum expected immediate post-refresh
Brier, breaking ties by lowest original question index. It then executes the
same realized first refresh, maximum-EIG second question, exact environment
reply, aligned terminal endpoint, validity penalties, and task-level common
random numbers as every other primary policy.

The selector consumes only conditioned branch supports already generated for
dynamic depth two. It adds no prompt, model call, candidate, branch, seed, or
realized path beyond the existing maximum of four distinct roots per task.

## Mandatory Mechanics

`myopic_refresh_brier` enters every existing primary-policy support, action,
likelihood-alignment, privacy, endpoint, and independent-replay check. Public
results report all four immediate refresh-Brier risks and its selected root.
No hidden benchmark truth enters selection.

## Mandatory Science Gates

All existing gates remain conjunctive. Add all of the following:

1. dynamic and `myopic_refresh_brier` roots differ on at least `16/64` tasks;
2. mean conditioned predicted terminal-Brier advantage of dynamic over the
   `myopic_refresh_brier`-selected root is at least `0.01`;
3. dynamic-minus-`myopic_refresh_brier` aligned realized terminal Brier is at
   most `-0.02`;
4. its 20,000-sample paired bootstrap probability of improvement is at least
   `0.90`;
5. terminal-Brier wins exceed losses;
6. dynamic mean aligned terminal log loss is no worse; and
7. on changed roots, predicted terminal-Brier advantage versus realized
   terminal-Brier advantage has Spearman at least `0.15` and bootstrap
   probability of positive correlation at least `0.80`.

`myopic_refresh_brier` is the headline horizon-isolating comparator.
`myopic_brier` remains the mandatory fixed-support matched-utility control and
`myopic_width` remains the mandatory entropy-EIG control. Failure of any new
gate makes development or confirmation a preregistered null.

## Reporting

The frozen report and paper table include all three myopic controls. Primary
interpretation, ranking fidelity, and the non-rescuing alignment-complete
diagnostic use dynamic versus `myopic_refresh_brier`. The fixed-support Brier
and entropy-EIG controls remain visible and required. No optional, secondary,
pooled, or subset result can rescue the full conjunction.

## Scope

This prospective amendment changes only selection and required comparisons.
It adds zero model calls and preserves prompts, supports, tasks, seeds,
budgets, request maxima, endpoints, and the four-root realized-path maximum.
Development and untouched confirmation use identical rules and thresholds.

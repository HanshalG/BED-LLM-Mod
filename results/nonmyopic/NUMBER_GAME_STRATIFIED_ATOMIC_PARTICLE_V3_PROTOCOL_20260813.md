# Number Game Stratified Atomic-Particle V3 Protocol

Date frozen: 2026-08-13, after terminal closure of atomic V2 and before any V3
response or implementation-informed model call.

## Motivation And Scientific Question

Atomic V2 established clean one-hypothesis transport but failed initial support
diversity: 53 executable particles represented only 15 extensions, with the
largest mode repeated 17 times. Soft family cues did not define disjoint modes.

V3 asks the same parent mechanics question while changing proposal generation
prospectively: can targeted, mutually exclusive answer-signature strata create a
stable path-dependent LLM particle belief whose depth-three ranking predicts and
improves realized canonical posterior quality?

This remains a mechanics stage. A pass authorizes only a separately frozen
powered development; a failure closes exact V3.

## Irreducible LLM Role

The LLM still proposes every name and executable rule. Code may specify a
target answer signature, parse and execute the response, reject noncompliance,
filter, resample, and score. It may not synthesize, repair, mutate, complete, or
patch a rule or any membership value. Duplicate extensions remain duplicate
particles within a stratum.

Target signatures are proposal strata, not posterior observations and not
candidate truths. They partition the open extension space; they never receive
weights based on canonical targets or endpoint outcomes. This is targeted
proposal generation in the sense used by BED-LLM, rather than a classical fixed
support bank.

## Deterministic Strata

The public anchor order is:

`7,12,25,42,81,5,16,33,64,90,2,9,20,50,75,3,14,27,60,99,1,6,18,40,70,4,11,30,55,88`

For each request group, define a protected set containing every query number
whose answer may be used to filter that group:

- initial: empty;
- conditioned refresh: the exact supplied history query numbers;
- first blind pool: all four candidate roots;
- second blind pool: all four roots and every dynamic or blind second query
  whose history will filter the pool.

The group's five anchors are the first five anchor-order values outside its
protected set. Enumerate all 32 five-bit signatures lexicographically from
`00000` through `11111`. Every prompt specifies its five anchors and required
YES/NO memberships, in addition to the real history when conditioned.

Each accepted expression must execute on all `0..100`, obey the real history,
and match all five stratum bits exactly. Different signatures on the same
anchors therefore imply different executable extensions by construction.

The frozen 416,366-extension grammar bank was opened once before this protocol
solely for a zero-call feasibility audit of the prospectively chosen initial
anchors `(7,12,25,42,81)`: all 32 signatures were present, with counts from
2,188 to 61,711. No model response, canonical target, policy score, or endpoint
was available. Because this audit influenced admission, V3 reports grammar
novelty descriptively but removes it from pass authority; the LLM-ownership gate
is instead exact independent-signature coverage plus the unchanged semantic
generation interface. The grammar bank still never enters a model prompt,
particle weight, policy, or endpoint.

## Particle And Policy Semantics

Every parent particle width, retained/generated 50/50 composition, systematic
resampling rule, candidate-root construction, dynamic depth-three score,
call-matched myopic, history-blind, fixed-support, deterministic-random control,
canonical-collapse penalty, half-bank audit, and response-before-endpoint rule
remains unchanged.

The original floors remain conjunctive:

- initial: at least 48 valid and 24 unique extensions per tree;
- conditioned 32-slot refresh: at least 24 valid and 16 unique;
- every branch-filtered blind pool: at least 24 valid and 16 unique.

Additionally, every accepted conditioned group must cover at least 24 distinct
signatures initially and 24 at each 32-slot refresh. Every branch-filtered blind
group must cover at least 16 signatures. These are measured after executable,
history, and signature validation; missing strata are never repaired.

## Frozen Gates

All parent transport, privacy, stability, calibration, ranking-fidelity, and
mechanics endpoint gates remain exact and conjunctive, except parent grammar
novelty gate 4 becomes descriptive for the disclosed ordering reason above.
V3 adds the signature-coverage gates stated above. In particular it still
requires:

- both disjoint half estimators agree with the full dynamic root on at least
  three trees;
- dynamic and call-matched-myopic roots differ on at least three trees and
  dynamic and blind roots differ on at least two;
- conditioned delayed MSE is at least 5% below blind and coverage at least five
  points higher;
- score-to-negative-realized-root-Brier Spearman is at least `.40`, with the
  selected root top two on at least three trees;
- dynamic canonical Brier is at least 3% below call-matched myopic, wins at least
  three tree means, and is nonworse than random; and
- dynamic is nonworse than blind on Brier and exact-extension coverage.

Grammar novelty and per-tree novelty are reported but cannot rescue or veto V3.

## Budget, Testing, And Failure

The immutable London-day `$5.00` account cap, `$3.50` V3 stage cap, exact
catalog revalidation, block reservation, per-request reauthorization, and
larger-of-posted-or-local reconciliation remain unchanged.

Before binding, synthetic/adversarial tests must prove signature serialization,
anchor protection, exact distinctness, noncompliance rejection, blind-pool
filtering, independent replay, all 6,400 request authorizations, success terminal,
and injected failed-closed terminal. Full Number Game tests must pass.

Any malformed, partial, budget-invalid, floor-invalid, replay-invalid, or gate-null
result is banked once and authorizes nothing. There is no retry, seed substitution,
signature change, threshold change, favorable-subset scoring, or response reuse.

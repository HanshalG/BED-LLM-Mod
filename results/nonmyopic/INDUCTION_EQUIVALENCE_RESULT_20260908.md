## Information-Access Decision

Treat the released formula-bank route as a potential calibration/reference
instrument, NOT the current LLM-native discovery headline. Its public bank is
small enough to enumerate. A study on those same planted concepts must include
the runnable public-library baseline with identical information access. It may
demonstrate exact sequential planning, but hiding the reference bank from the
classical baseline would not demonstrate a useful LLM-generated hypothesis space.

This is an epistemic limitation, not a claim that an LLM could never outperform
a particular uniform-library predictor. A learned prior, better computation or
misspecification can change performance; those would require their own matched
controls and clearly specified estimands. Do not confuse them with discovering
previously unavailable structures.

Stop the released-bank audit chain here rather than building a new adapter just
because it is easy. We already have numerical reference instruments; another one
does not fill the missing discovery-plus-planning evidence. Further syntactic
normalization or source-family inventory would not resolve public-library access.

Any continuation using this language must be a separately and prospectively
defined NEW-concept experiment with a reproducible non-tiny model distribution,
not the ten-template generator or a renamed held-out slice of the public bank.
It must be labelled a new active extension, not reproduction of INDUCTION's
private release pipeline. Its generative law and query distribution must be
independent of observed planning gains and equally accessible to symbolic and
LLM proposals. That contract, useful proposal evidence and complete numerical
opportunity remain missing. No such experiment is authorized by this audit.

No additional dataset search, parser platform, paid call or unchanged SciLaws
microvariant follows automatically. The next architecture decision must address
the missing non-tiny discovery distribution directly; finite-suite split cleanup
is no longer the bottleneck. Existing scene/ACRE/Number Game closures stand.

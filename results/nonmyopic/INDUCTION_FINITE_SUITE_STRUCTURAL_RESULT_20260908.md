## Consequence For The Architecture

The released suite is materially different from the ten-template public
generator. The prior audit must not be used to reject it as that generator.
However, 135 known strings is also an easily enumerated oracle library. An
oracle planning gain using this library cannot demonstrate useful LLM discovery.
Conversely, finite benchmark size alone does not prove that inference on novel
concepts is easy. Those are different questions.

The finite-suite route remains a candidate for an end-to-end instrument, not a
qualified headline environment. Proceed next only with a bounded semantic/split
and information-access contract, reusing upstream formula semantics. Do not
rebuild the planner. In particular:

1. Check alpha/Boolean-equivalent and source-family overlap before sealing any
   empirical concept-generalization split. Keep all equivalent forms in one
   group; do not optimize split sizes or gains by trying salts.
2. Distinguish the public-library diagnostic (available equally to classical and
   LLM arms) from genuine held-out-concept synthesis. If benchmark-public formulas
   make discovery a lookup exercise, do not hide them from a classical control
   to manufacture LLM advantage. A publishable new-concept claim needs a separate
   prospective source-supported distribution, not a renamed subset of this bank.
3. Candidate worlds and fixed target worlds must be generated independently of
   the private concept. Existing released worlds were selected under their own
   construction process; their geometry is not automatically label-independent.
4. A complete exact binary h1/h2/h3 plus open-loop and random diagnostic must be
   frozen before membership responses, and finish under the existing practical
   budget. Its oracle gain remains separate from deployable-prior opportunity.

This is not permission to start another large source search, change closed
SciLaws numerical gates, or run a paid proposer. The immediate unresolved issue
is whether a fair information contract leaves a meaningful discovery problem,
not whether another parser can be made to pass tests. If it does not, retain this
suite as a calibration instrument only and do not promote it to the full goal.

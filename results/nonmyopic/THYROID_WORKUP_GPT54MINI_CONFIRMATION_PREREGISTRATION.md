# UCI Thyroid GPT-5.4 Mini Trajectory Transfer Preregistration

Status: frozen after the Gemma 4 26B trajectory serving failure and before any
GPT-5.4 Mini response from this thyroid interface is observed.

This is a cross-model transfer, not a repair or rerun of the stopped 26B line. It
uses fresh seeds and retains the same machine-fixed roots, named branch-policy
schema, exact verifier, posterior, controls, endpoints, and thresholds. The model is
non-thinking; reasoning is reserved for the separate naive-thinking baseline.

## S0 late-state robustness smoke

- Seed `24157`; 12 mechanics-only cells.
- Cells are generated by exact depth-two histories at lengths 0,1,2,3,4,5,6,6,5,4,3,2.
  They therefore include early collection opportunities and late states with
  shrinking legal menus and already-queried assays, the case that stopped 26B.
- Model: `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` via OpenRouter, non-thinking, temperature zero,
  1,024-token output cap, one bounded validation-feedback retry.
- Required: all 12 accepted; history lengths 0--6 covered; every named follow-up
  complete and legal; no policy repeats its root as a follow-up; zero reasoning,
  forced exits, and rollout/scoring calls.
- S0 choices are mechanics-only and never quality evidence. Any failure stops this
  model line; there is no parser repair.

## Conditional S1 paired confirmation

S1 is launched only if S0 passes every mechanic.

- Fresh seed `24158`; 50 patient rows without replacement; eight paired actions.
- Arms: GPT-5.4 Mini named depth-two continuations, matched-random named
  continuations on identical machine construction, exact depth one, and exhaustive
  depth two. All arms use an exact one-step final action in round 8.
- Ten thousand paired bootstraps. The primary endpoint is mean post-action target
  entropy AUC; truth-log probability AUC is the truth-anchored corroboration.
- Exactly 350 accepted logical model cells are required.

Frozen S1 gates, all required:

1. Positive paired 95% lower bounds for GPT entropy-AUC and truth-log-AUC gains over exact depth one.
2. Positive paired 95% lower bounds for GPT entropy-AUC and truth-log-AUC gains over matched random.
3. Mean GPT entropy gain over exact depth one recovers at least 60% of the mean exhaustive-depth-two gain.
4. GPT selects blood collection first on at least 75% of trajectories.
5. Fifty distinct paired truths, complete legal traces, exactly 350 accepted logical cells, and zero reasoning, forced exits, and scoring-time LLM calls.

Failure after the one registered retry stops S1 without replacement. A pass requires
an independent full replay before banking.

The run budget is capped by the existing `$6` OpenRouter per-run guard and the
project ceiling of `$110`. Based on prior GPT-5.4 Mini runs, S0 plus S1 is expected
to cost well below the remaining `$75.54` headroom.

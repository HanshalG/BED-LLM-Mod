# Bongard OpenWorld Classical Claim-Scope Amendment

Frozen: 2026-08-09 (Europe/London), before any Bongard mechanics,
development, confirmation, candidate, or endpoint outcome was opened.

Status: **implemented and fail-closed; zero model calls and zero outcomes**.

This amendment changes only result-contingent paper scope. It changes no Luna
model, request, prompt, response schema, task, seed, action, endpoint, policy,
score, scientific gate, result tier, call count, or budget.

## Problem

The mandatory classical-suite adapter already reports paired Luna-minus-DINO
and Luna-minus-SigLIP effects in every direction. However, paper-wrapper V1
preserved the Luna-only abstract and contribution macros byte-for-byte. A full
within-Luna confirmation could therefore populate the same headline whether
Luna clearly beat both fixed semantic-vision planners or failed to do so.

That is too implicit for the project's central distinction. A positive
within-Luna control establishes useful path-dependent LLM belief dynamics; it
does not by itself establish that the Bongard task requires an LLM rather than
a fixed pretrained representation.

## Frozen Classical Challenge

For each of DINOv2 depth two and SigLIP2 depth two, use the already registered
task-paired Luna-dynamic-minus-classical summaries. A challenger is cleared
only when all three conditions hold:

1. mean endpoint-Brier difference is below zero;
2. the upper endpoint of the two-sided 95% 20,000-draw task-bootstrap interval
   is below zero; and
3. mean endpoint log-loss difference is nonpositive.

Luna clears the joint classical challenge only if both individual challengers
clear. This is an intersection-union claim: requiring both comparisons avoids
selecting the easier classical baseline after outcomes. Accuracy and truth
probability remain mandatory direction-agnostic reports but do not enter this
scope label.

## Deterministic Headline Mapping

The original Luna claim tier remains unchanged and is never rescued or vetoed
by this amendment.

- Development, confirmation null, and mechanics failure retain empty headline
  macros exactly.
- A literal `full_llm_native_confirmation` that clears both classical
  challengers keeps its registered within-Luna text and appends that Luna also
  beat both frozen fixed semantic-vision depth-two planners under the criterion
  above.
- A literal `full_llm_native_confirmation` that does not clear both keeps its
  registered within-Luna text but must append that the result is limited to
  within-Luna path-dependent belief dynamics and does not establish task-level
  LLM necessity.

Thus an unfavorable classical result cannot disappear into a late body
paragraph, while a fixed semantic baseline cannot alter the prospectively
registered within-Luna causal estimand.

Missing comparisons, missing log loss, non-finite values, malformed intervals,
the wrong bootstrap count, unknown scope, or an authorized headline outside
full confirmation fail closed. The complete scope object is written to paper
metadata.

all paid behavior remain byte-identical.

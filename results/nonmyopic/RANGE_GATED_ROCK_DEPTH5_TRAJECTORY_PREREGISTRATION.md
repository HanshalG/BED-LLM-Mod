## Frozen Environment And Policy

- Standard RockSample[7,8], start `(6,6)`.
- Range-gated sensor: `.55` remote accuracy and `.95` on-site accuracy.
- Focused independent prior: rock 6 has `p_good=.5`; every other rock has
  `p_good=.005`.
- Eight real rounds and 50 paired truths sampled from that prior.
- Model: thinking `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, temperature zero.
- Hierarchical policy:
  - machine fixes two movement and two exact-d1 check roots;
  - the LLM assigns four distinct semantic rock targets;
  - a deterministic compiler takes three shortest-path transit slots and checks
    the target;
  - exact h5 scores the four compiled plans and executes only the selected root;
  - when fewer than five real rounds remain, the arm uses exact truncated
    planning and makes no LLM call.
- Prompts expose uncertainty and geometry but no EIG, values, ranks, routes, or
  actions.
- Exact prompt digests cache repeated cells. Every logical decision is retained;
  at most 64 unique physical proposal cells are allowed.
- Serving uses 4,096 reasoning tokens, one bounded 128-token non-reasoning final
  when needed, one validation retry, JSON-prefix decoding from character zero,
  and complete request accounting.
- Projection: none.

## Paired Arms

All arms share truth indices and deterministic observation uniforms.

- `llm_h5`: cached hierarchical targets scored at h5.
- `shared_h4`: the identical hierarchical h5 plans selected using their h4
  prefixes, then executing the selected full plan's root.
- `random_h5`: four random distinct targets with identical roots, compiler, and
  exact h5 verifier.
- `exact_d4`: exhaustive receding-horizon d4.
- `exact_d5`: exhaustive receding-horizon d5 opportunity ceiling.

The primary endpoints are posterior-entropy AUC gains of `llm_h5` over
`shared_h4`, `random_h5`, and `exact_d4`. Truth-log-posterior AUC gains against
the same three controls are co-primary corroboration. Secondary endpoints are
exact d5-gap recovery, registered-route rate, on-site check rate by round five,
final entropy, truth log probability, and MAP accuracy.

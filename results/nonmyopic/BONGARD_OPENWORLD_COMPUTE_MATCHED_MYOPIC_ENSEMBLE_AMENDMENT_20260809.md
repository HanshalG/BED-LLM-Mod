# Bongard Compute-Matched Myopic Ensemble Amendment

Frozen: 2026-08-09 (Europe/London), before any Bongard mechanics,
development, or confirmation response and before any candidate or endpoint
outcome was opened.

This amendment adds a stronger myopic control using responses already required
by the frozen matched history-blind design. It changes no task, image, prompt,
model, requested seed, response schema, branch request, endpoint, or daily cost
ceiling.

## Control

For each task, `compute_matched_myopic_ensemble` uses exactly 17 Luna belief
responses:

- the ordinary root belief; and
- all 16 same-history beliefs generated by the existing history-blind calls.

Every one of the 16 added beliefs is generated from the byte-identical ordinary
root prompt under a distinct requested seed. For each belief independently, the
control computes one-step endpoint predictive information gain for all eight
candidate queries. Its root score is the arithmetic mean of those 17 one-step
score maps. It selects the deterministic maximum.

After observing the selected query's realized label, the control uses the same
answer-conditioned dynamic branch updater and greedy second query as the
ordinary myopic-width arm. Its terminal belief uses the same algorithm,
task-level seed, dispatch batch, and sealed endpoint scorer as every other arm.
The frozen all-first-action terminal bank already contains its trajectory, so
the control adds no terminal request.

## Compute Contract

The dynamic planner consumes one root response plus 16 answer-conditioned
branch responses for first-query scoring. The new control consumes one root
response plus 16 answer-free root-prompt responses. Both therefore use 17 Luna
belief generations, the same per-response token limit and schema, and one
realized terminal update. The comparison asks whether conditioning those 16
extra calls on counterfactual answers is more useful than spending the same
calls reducing current-belief generation noise.

The existing shuffled-continuation control remains a complementary test of
branch-to-action coupling. The ordinary myopic-width arm remains the cheaper
online greedy baseline.

## Development Gates

The positive policy family and complete development conjunction additionally
require:

- at least 24 of 64 final histories differ from the new control;
- at least 24 first-query changes clear the numerical tie margin under both
  score maps;
- at least one changed final history in every 16-task execution block;
- at least 3% relative endpoint-Brier improvement;
- paired bootstrap probability of Brier improvement at least 0.80;
- mean endpoint log loss no worse; and
- dynamic mean first-action ranking Spearman no worse.

## Confirmation Gates

The positive confirmation conjunction additionally requires:

- at least 36 of 96 final histories differ;
- at least 36 robust first-query changes;
- at least one changed history in every 24-task block;
- at least 3% relative endpoint-Brier improvement;
- paired task-bootstrap 95% upper bound for dynamic-minus-control Brier below
  zero;
- mean endpoint log loss no worse; and
- dynamic mean first-action ranking Spearman no worse.

Failure of any new gate makes the corresponding complete conjunction null. The
control cannot rescue a failed existing gate, authorize an endpoint, or change
the interpretation of a partial tier.

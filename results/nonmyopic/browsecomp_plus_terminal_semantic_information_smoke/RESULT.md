# BrowseComp-Plus Terminal Semantic Information Smoke

## Result

The preregistered all-root terminal-distribution smoke passes every serving and
scientific gate on the five already-open mechanics tasks.

GPT-5.4 nonreasoning completed exactly 30 fresh terminal-belief calls with no
retry, reasoning token, forced exit, repair, or reissue. The run cost
`$0.2094175`.

Target-blind semantic entropy scores achieve:

- total entropy drop vs total truth-mass gain: 0.8475 over 59 pairs;
- total entropy drop vs total evidence: 0.8571 over 42 pairs;
- total entropy drop vs total gold evidence: 0.9118 over 34 pairs;
- future entropy drop vs future truth-mass gain: 0.6949 over 59 pairs; and
- future entropy drop vs future evidence gain: 0.6842 over 38 pairs.

The depth-2 entropy policy changes the myopic entropy root on two of five tasks.
It wins terminal truth mass on two tasks and total evidence on one, with no loss
on either endpoint.

The cleanest change is task 286. Depth 2 selects root 2 instead of root 5,
raising total evidence from 2 to 3, gold evidence from 1 to 2, and terminal
truth mass from 0.9245 to 0.9600. On task 747 it recovers a small truth mass
(0 to 0.0612) without changing evidence. The remaining three selections tie.

## Interpretation

This is a mechanics pass for an output-distribution value estimator. It
supports the causal diagnosis from the cached audit: observation-conditioned
belief distributions carry useful ranking signal, while explicit absolute
future-value judgments did not.

The smoke remains post hoc on open mechanics tasks. It does not establish an
out-of-sample policy improvement. It authorizes only the separately
preregistered small untouched validation.

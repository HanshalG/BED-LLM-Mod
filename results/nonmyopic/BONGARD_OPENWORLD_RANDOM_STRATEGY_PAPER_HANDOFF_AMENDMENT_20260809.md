# Bongard OpenWorld Random-Strategy Paper Handoff Amendment

Frozen: 2026-08-09, before any August 10 Bongard model response, candidate
label, endpoint label, or terminal artifact was opened.

Status: **prospective zero-call reporting amendment; authorizes no model
request**.

## Problem

The frozen paid protocol executes a task-hashed random two-query policy, and the
prospective random-strategy audit independently replays its choices and paired
endpoint effects. The existing mandatory paper wrapper names random selection
as a control but does not require the audit artifact or render its result. That
leaves a direction-dependent omission path after outcomes are known.

## Mandatory Handoff

Paper-wrapper V4 must require, independently replay, and bind the exact frozen
random-strategy audit whenever a development or confirmation endpoint result is
rendered. It must reject a missing audit, stage mismatch, stage-result mismatch,
failed authorization, changed protocol or implementation, malformed summary,
non-finite value, wrong task count, non-20,000-draw bootstrap, or inconsistent
standard error.

The generated body must report, for all tasks in the rendered stage:

- dynamic-depth-two minus random mean Brier difference and paired 95% interval;
- dynamic-depth-two minus random mean log-loss difference and paired 95%
  interval;
- Brier wins, ties, and losses;
- first-query and final-history change counts; and
- the explicit qualification that random is a descriptive sanity baseline and
  is not compute matched.

The paragraph is mandatory regardless of effect direction. A mechanics failure
has no endpoint random audit and must reject one if supplied.

## Supersession

Paper-wrapper V4 replaces V3 as the mandatory final development and
confirmation rendering path. It preserves the V3 Luna fragment, DINOv2 and
SigLIP2 suite, compute-matched audit, classical headline scope, and all
result-tier mappings, while adding the required random-audit replay and body
paragraph. Direct publication from V3 is scientifically incomplete after this
amendment.

## Claim Boundary

This amendment changes no model, reasoning mode, prompt, image, task, split,
seed, candidate, endpoint, policy, likelihood, action, updater, request count,
cost cap, scientific gate, claim tier, confirmation authorization, classical
scope, headline, or result interpretation. Dynamic beating random cannot
establish non-myopia, and losing to random cannot modify a frozen tier; both
directions must simply remain visible beside the causal and compute-matched
controls.

The wrapper and audit make zero model calls, cost `$0`, and authorize no paid
call, rerun, repair, descendant, endpoint opening, or claim.

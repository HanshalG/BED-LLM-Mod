# Positioning Notes

## Constrained BED via Online Planning

Guo et al. (2026), "Constrained Bayesian Experimental Design via Online Planning" ([arXiv:2605.26990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26990)), is directly adjacent: it studies constrained sequential BED, including a transition-constrained location-finding task where consecutive designs have limited movement, and proposes COPEx, a semi-amortized online planner that uses learned posterior/predictive surrogates plus scenario-tree lookahead. This means our constrained location result should not be framed as discovering a new classical planning problem; COPEx already establishes that online multi-step planning helps under these constraints. Our differentiation is the general-purpose LLM/NL-strategy scaffold: natural-language strategies are generated, evolved, and rollout-scored without task-specific policy training or an amortized posterior network, with ranking-fidelity diagnostics measuring whether the LLM strategy scorer is actually calibrated to realized gain. The strongest workshop framing is therefore: COPEx is a specialized constrained-BED planner; StrategyEIG asks whether LLM-generated strategy policies can recover part of the greedy-to-oracle gap at roughly linear-in-depth strategy-evaluation cost, while remaining portable to natural-language experimental-design tasks such as 20Q where classical continuous planners do not directly apply.
